## Hi! 
I'm a PhD student at Berkeley and NSF GRFP fellow, working at the intersection of ML and scientific design (proteins, materials). I'm advised by [Jennifer Listgarten](http://www.jennifer.listgarten.com/) and [Sergey Levine](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~svlevine/). These days, I'm most interested in understanding how domain-specific aspects of the scientific design problems I work on interact with various models and design algorithms. How might we allow scientists to more completely specify the details of their problems and what exactly they'd like to get out of a design algorithm?

During my undergrad (Caltech), I primarily spent time thinking about adaptive experiment design, Bayesian inference, GPs, and uncertainty quantification in [Yisong Yue's](https://www.yisongyue.com/) group. I also had a lot of fun [teaching](/pages/teaching/) for a variety of the core CS / ML courses!
