Hi! I'm a PhD student at Berkeley and NSF GRFP fellow, working at the intersection of ML and scientific design (proteins, materials). I'm advised by [Jennifer Listgarten](http://www.jennifer.listgarten.com/) and [Sergey Levine](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~svlevine/). These days, I'm most interested in understanding how domain-specific aspects of the scientific design problems I work on interact with various models and design algorithms.

During my undergrad (Caltech), I primarily spent time thinking about adaptive experiment design, Bayesian inference, and GPs and uncertainty quantification in [Yisong Yue's](https://www.yisongyue.com/) group. I also had a lot of fun [teaching](/pages/teaching/) for a variety of the core CS / ML courses!
