I get on plane soon! I'm really very tired. Genuine exhaustion. Life makes me sad. All nice things are taken away far too quickly for my taste.

["The Worm King's Lullaby, Richard Silken](https://genius.com/Richard-siken-the-worm-kings-lullaby-annotated)
