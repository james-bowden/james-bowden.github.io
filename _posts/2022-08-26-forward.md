["My Life Was the Size of My Life", Jane Hirshfield](https://poets.org/poem/my-life-was-size-my-life)
> Well done personification poem.

From Caleb Nelson: *It's summer now, and you're craving a simpler existence.* Indeed, I am.<br/>
*You're looking forward to forgetting, albeit briefly, the existential dread which plagues you, which tightens your chest, which pains your left side.* I am.<br/>
*You're looking forward to freedom, even if it is short, even if it might not last. / You're looking forward.* It is the last part that is important. I am not sure how there I am.<br/>

["Tomorrow I'm Going to Rewrite the English Language", Lois Keith](https://www.martialartsplanet.com/threads/disability-and-language.90178/)
> This feels good and powerful. Newspeak. But I mean, yes.
