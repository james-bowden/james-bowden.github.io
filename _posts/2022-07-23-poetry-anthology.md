So I compiled this anthology at the end of last term (spring 2022) as a part of En85 at Caltech, and wanted to give it a home of sorts linked somewhere from my website, and not just drifting in space. The image that goes with this is of a button/pin thingy that Antonia made me, which is a reference to a famous Rilke poem included in the anthology called "Archaic Torso of Apollo". I love dearly everything therein, and that is enough, though you're welcome to as well. Or to not. Anyhow, here it is: [*Having a Poem with You*, a poetry anthology](https://james-bowden.github.io/pages/anthology/)
