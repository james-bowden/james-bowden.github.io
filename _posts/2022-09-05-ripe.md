["Dinosaurs Smelled Magnolias", Dalton Day](https://www.reddit.com/r/Poetry/comments/ou152h/poem_dinosaurs_smelled_magnolias_by_dalton_day/)
> I've come across this before, but it's nice. *& so I do & so I do*. Funnily, this poem seems to be popular among Reddit and Tumblr-type communities, but doesn't have any prints online from the usual sources.

Here's a little excerpt from Lucia LoTempio:<br/>
*I loved a man. I loved. I don't know how else to begin.*

And some from Izumi Shikibu:<br/>
*My body--so used to you. Even to think of forgetting, too sad to consider.*

And then, a favorite from Ocean:<br/>
*I miss you more than I remember you*. Always this line. Somtimes I wonder if this is even possible, but surely it is. It just implies that missing has little to do with knowledge. And so here we sit.

From John Cage, a love letter:<br/>
*Please kiss whatever part of you you can reach for me.* I humored it, and tried it. Was nice. A kiss has inherent gentleness, no?
