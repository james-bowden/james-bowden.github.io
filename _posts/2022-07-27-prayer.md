["Late Prayer", Jane Hirshfield](https://ruahbull.blogspot.com/2022/06/late-prayer-jane-hirshfield.html)
> *Tenderness does not choose its own uses*. I'm going to follow this with some of my prayer poems simply because this reminded me of them. These are some of the shorter pieces that I've written, I suppose.

["Recognizance Prayer", James Bowden](https://james-bowden.github.io/pages/poetry/jb_recognizance)

["Serenity Prayer", James Bowden](https://james-bowden.github.io/pages/poetry/jb_serenity)

["UCB Prayer", James Bowden](https://james-bowden.github.io/pages/poetry/jb_ucb)
> This one is based largely on vocabulary from Anne Sexton's ["Jesus Suckles"](https://james-bowden.github.io/pages/poetry/as_truck), so enjoy that first perhaps.

["Gravy", Raymond Carver](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/1988/08/29/gravy)
> *That's what it was. Gravy.* This reminds me of what I was talking about in one of the recent blogs, about affirmations that can be repeated like *this is the honey* and *this is water* and *this is the gravy*. I just love the quality that gravy evokes in my mind. More savory than honey and water. More drawn out to the last drop, licked clean in quality.

["things people like to share:", Nuar Alsadir](https://yalereview.org/article/nuar-alsadir-poem-share)

["What's a bucket?", Eve Blank and James Bowden](https://james-bowden.github.io/pages/poetry/jb_bucket)
> A first, a collaborative poem that I think turned out quite productive. Entrez le déluge. Enjoy.
