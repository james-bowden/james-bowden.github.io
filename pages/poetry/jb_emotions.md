## ~ emotions ~
#### James Bowden

What the hell do I feel?<br/>

Hot. Or at least that's what I've been telling everyone who asks me.<br/>
That's certainly what's at the forefront of my mind as I flee the broken AC of my stuffy apartment<br/>
to take refuge among the familiar red and white stripes and grease and dying palm trees.<br/>
That's certainly what I told my hairdresser. And my roommate. And my Lyft driver. And my cousin.<br/>
And my PSTP friends.<br/>

I don't feel fucking anything. Or maybe I feel everything.<br/>
Is it possible to become desensitized to one's own emotions?<br/>

I always thought my dad was a man of few emotions.<br/>
My mom disagreed. She said he locked them up deep inside his brain and threw the key away.<br/>
That he feared them because they controlled him. Overwhelmed him. That they haunt him. <br/>
Undoubtedly a great recipe for a happy family.<br/>

Is it even possible to be fully in control of one's emotions?<br/>
Doesn't that conflict with the very fucking definition of emotion?<br/>
Raw feeling. An output given an input.<br/>
Yeah, yeah, yeah. We can control how we interpret and act upon them.<br/>
The raw feeling is there all the same. What better excuse is there than innateness?<br/>

Don't lie to me, you childish idiot. You're no robot.<br/>
Perhaps an overloaded heap of grey matter trying to process too many outputs.<br/>
Trying. Failing.<br/>
Why do I feel so fucking binary?<br/>
Either react to every emotion or to none.<br/>
All or nothing. And now I am that which I most resented in my father. The better I get to know<br/>
myself, the better I understand him. Maybe we’re all control freaks because of what it means to <br/>
have been a child.<br/>

Cold. I've never told anyone that.<br/>
But we all know that eventually the heat must give way and what else can follow?<br/>
My heat death. <br/>
Who'd have thought?<br/>
Does that make sweating out of my ass like a dog worth savoring the same way we lie to ourselves<br/>
and try to appreciate the brevity of this life?<br/>
Fuck being refined. I want to be raw. I want to go supernova. Not fucking red dwarf.<br/>
And yet the very definition once again highlights how temporary it all is. I can't handle the idea of<br/>
not being eternal. <br/>

I want to be dynamic. I swear. Or at least not static.<br/>
I want to be forever. Definitely don't have to swear that one to convince myself.<br/>
I want to be alive. And feel it too. <br/>

Orwell is brilliant. Yeah, the Party was fucked up. But I could get behind a NewSpeak in which all<br/>
words expressing infinity, eternity, forever are gone. I wish I could unlearn the concept of<br/>
limitlessness.<br/>
That's a lie. As if we ever really have a choice to take the blue pill.<br/>

*Piso mojado. <br/>
Cuídate,* mortal. <br/>
Your actions. Your mistakes. Your *feelings*. They will live forever. <br/>
Just like you, right? What a joke. <br/>
If only I could convince myself that everything that I do, that I am, that I stand for, that I feel, dies with me.<br/>

I'm a quarter of the way through my life, for Christ's sake.<br/>

How am I afraid to live with the consequences of my actions?<br/>
I know that I'm more afraid to live with the consequences of my inaction. But there's always a<br/>
tomorrow, right? Always potential for better. Maybe it'll be easier in the future. Clearer.<br/>
You're fucking delusional. <br/>
Live every day like it's your last. Every minute. <br/>
You say it, you think it, it's your goddamned wallpaper, and yet you can't.<br/>

*If you think just maybe it's her<br/>
Promise me, young man, you're gonna let her know.*<br/>
Yeah, fuck you too Dermot. I've still got a whole life left to live. Fool.<br/>

Why am I still terrified?<br/>
Perhaps alive is a better adjective.<br/>
Perhaps it will be brief. Most certainly. Flare and then pull a Warren G. Harding. Doesn't it always?<br/>
But would I want it any other way?<br/>
Composure, control, it's all overrated. Hollow.<br/>
I'd rather be a hot mess than a damned icicle. Real life isn't binary. It's sloppy. It's here and now.<br/>
What an apt place to be writing this.<br/>
It's never been about the outcome, but the raw feeling.<br/>
Who am I kidding. I know which matters more to me. <br/>

But it's time. It always is, or it never is.<br/>

<hr>

So interestingly, this poem serves as a precursor of sorts for "The world isn't ready for my love", and "Having a Boba with You", in that even though it's mostly not a love poem at all, the last bit I used to tell a girl I had feelings for. Again, no intentions: we were off to college in different places, she had a boyfriend, etc., just thought it would be nice to tell someone that they were loved.

You also get some of this reference style. The lines are from Dermot Kennedy's [After Rain](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KrxqoCWDPdo). The reference to Warren G. Harding is because when we learned about him in high school, the main policy he had was "return to normalcy". Orwell reference is to *1984*. And of course, this all takes place in a good ol' In-N-Out.

With this, and a lot of the other earlier poems, you don't really get the sense of gentleness that I feel has emerged in my poems from this past year or so. I think I was a lot harsher on myself at this point, hadn't really learned to love myself or life or anything really. I don't find them very compelling in large part because of this, which is consistent with what Jenny would tell me.
