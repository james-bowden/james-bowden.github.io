## *How I go to the woods*
#### Mary Oliver

Ordinarily, I go to the woods alone, with not a single<br/>
friend, for they are all smilers and talkers and therefore<br/>
unsuitable.<br/>

I don’t really want to be witnessed talking to the catbirds<br/>
or hugging the old black oak tree. I have my way of<br/>
praying, as you no doubt have yours.<br/>

Besides, when I am alone I can become invisible. I can sit<br/>
on the top of a dune as motionless as an uprise of weeds,<br/>
until the foxes run by unconcerned. I can hear the almost<br/>
unhearable sound of the roses singing.<br/>

If you have ever gone to the woods with me, I must love<br/>
you very much.<br/>
