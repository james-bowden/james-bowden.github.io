## *I Don't Want to Live a Small Life*
#### Mary Oliver

I don't want to live a small life. Open your eyes,<br/>
open your hands. I have just come<br/>
from the berry fields, the sun<br/>

kissing me with its golden mouth all the way<br/>
(open your hands) and the wind-winged clouds<br/>
following along thinking perhaps I might<br/>

feed them, but no I carry these heart-shapes<br/>
only to you. Look how many small<br/>
but so sweet and maybe the last gift<br/>

I will bring to anyone in this<br/>
world of hope and risk, so do.<br/>
Look at me. Open your life, open your hands.<br/>
