## Smoothness Study
#### James Bowden

I wouldn't say that I'm confused, more just don't know<br/>
which way to look with all this information consuming<br/>
me at all times. How many Hz is my brain running at?<br/>
Would a hummingbird, a mosquito be better equipped<br/>
to take my place? I would think that in every creature's<br/>
head, it must be a smooth operator. Imagine being a bug,<br/>
a bumblebee perhaps, all jerky and out of control. Oh.<br/>
Counterexample. Well, in my head, at least, processes run<br/>
quickly enough to leave me bored but slowly enough to<br/>
leave me overwhelmed when I do try. There are so many<br/>
dimensions to existence, and I want all of them. Though<br/>
lately everything has felt like prisoners' dilemmas. I try<br/>
to reach out but you defect before I can even make contact.<br/>
I mean, maybe fair. I've not really \[been\] shown that I've value<br/>
for just existing. What can I offer you? There are flowers<br/>
on the floor. Our intuitia suggest something depressing,<br/>
but no: they're simply growing there. I don't know why,<br/>
but they're glorious and it's morning anyway. What's that?<br/>
Oh yes, it seems you're involved now. My apologies. Reading<br/>
poetry requires an eye from a trusted thing and an eye<br/>
from an unquilted thing. If you've only one of those at present,<br/>
*here, let me help you*, there, quilt's off, there are flowers on<br/>
the floor! Corn. What I've been looking for, hoping for,<br/>
is someOne or someThing greater than me so that I may be<br/>
Held, Fully Understood. Uh oh, looks like you're involved now!<br/>
Someone to simulate me differentiably and manipulate me<br/>
good. I realized that my old basis was bad. So I constructed<br/>
a new one from all the people around me I could love. But<br/>
here's the prisoners' dilemma again, people defect, my basis<br/>
is strong but not robust nor stable, so back to the drawing board.<br/>
Looks like you must be uninvolved now. *Here, let me help you:*<br/>
I would like to spit in \<redacted\>'s hand once again.<br/>
Okay, maybe that was overkill. I was weaned on the school of<br/>
*seeing optimal, believing in optimization, but not being able*<br/>
*to optimize all things worth optimizing*. Now I'm getting my<br/>
PhD in machine learning. I don't think I'll ever learn. Some<br/>
day I'll love Frank O'Hara and Ocean Vuong, but for now<br/>
I think I'll post about them on Instagram. Enter dopamine<br/>
rush for virtual people caring about me. *To*day I love James<br/>
Bowden! I feel like I'm killing romance, leaving no space<br/>
for it in my life. I'm trying to lay my shoulders bare but<br/>
keep getting slapped for it. One power I never fail to<br/>
attribute people: the power to repress. Perhaps<br/>
much of what we're asking for in companionship is<br/>
just for self destruction to be made a little<br/>
less easy. Reenter the real world. It's grey and bleak outside<br/>
the window behind me and I shall soon be forced to depart.<br/>
The trees are lovely and I've been allowed to wear a long-<br/>
sleeve. I'm really not that unhappy, ever, in the present.<br/>
Only when my present stretches out to encompass the past<br/>
and the dreaded future which of course, by the Markov property,<br/>
will just be the present and happy enough if I can manage not to<br/>
think about the future future when I get there. Maybe my brain<br/>
just wants all of its functions to be smooth. Namely, its model<br/>
of the world. Its model of my trajectory. Call that<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;security? Yesterday, the past and future<br/>
invaded my sleep and lent me a dream of everyday (quite ordinary)<br/>
terror (nothing special). I woke shaking and screamt into the void,<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;just let me be dead for a little while,<br/>
I will come back. I just can't bear the weight of my entire lifetime at<br/>
each<br/>
single<br/>
moment.<br/>
