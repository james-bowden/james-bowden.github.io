## At the California Institute of Technology
#### James Bowden 

I don't care how God-damn sexy<br/>
their technology is: I'm nauseous.<br/>

The Romans created sewage systems eons ago<br/>
and the guy next door refuses to flush the toilet.<br/>

<hr>

This takes its form and inspiration from Richard Brautigan's ["At the California Institute of Technology"](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/48578/at-the-california-institute-of-technology). He was poet-in-residence at Caltech! The odds! This also reminds me about how a large part of Bill Gates' polio eradication campaign was literally just installed sewage systems in all of these third world countries. Jesus.
