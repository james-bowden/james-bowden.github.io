## my wall full of trinkets taped to my wall (III)
#### James Bowden 

you'd be surprised what 59¢ can buy:<br/>
forgiveness, and with the change,<br/>
an envelope of frail poppies (and *heirloom* variety, at that)<br/>
to be washed out by spring's first showers--<br/>

just in time for crowded birthday clovers<br/>
to cope with the pot of weeds I'd watered<br/>
that weren't waiting when I went home<br/>
sans you, sans him, sans her.<br/>

don't worry, they're dying <br/>
to be torn out and trapped in turquoise tape too--<br/>
