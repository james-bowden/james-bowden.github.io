## *Sun Valley Lodge*
#### Ellie Sawatzky

Somewhere in my mind, you've become<br/>
inseparable from the old boats, the plastic<br/>
Adirondack chairs. Listen, I saw plastic<br/>
Adirondack chairs long before I ever<br/>
saw you. I also saw red cars, fireweed,<br/>
deer in the road. I saw ukeleles and *Amelie*.<br/>
I rolled my own cigarettes, smoked them<br/>
alone, I crocheted poems to keep warm<br/>
at night before you, so step back, love,<br/>
this is my line of pine trees. This is my<br/>
soft light falling fast behind the lake.<br/>
