## lactose intolerant on a plane from newark
#### James Bowden

honey, don’t worry,<br/>
all things pass as gas,<br/>
we’ve rilke to thank for that,<br/>
even if you’re clutched over<br/>
in pain for the moment,<br/>
none of it, none of it<br/>
persists, just you. <br/>
just you, baby. <br/>
