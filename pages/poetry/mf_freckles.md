## A Simple Love Poem
#### Megan Falley

It is only September.<br/>
I don’t know how many seasons<br/>
I will be allowed to love you yet.<br/>
What I do know is that you have flown<br/>
one thousand Miles to stand in my kitchen,<br/>
dropping chocolate chips into pumpkin pancakes<br/>
like arranging freckles for the face of a perfect child.<br/>
Feeding me the extra semisweet moles.<br/>
I don’t yet know for how many years<br/>
you might flip me pancakes for.<br/>
If you will still love me when that blonde tree<br/>
sheds her Hollywood wig. If we will make it<br/>
to the season of the blueberries, but I don’t care.<br/>
The tree is a pin-up girl posing outside my window<br/>
and you’re only looking at me. Every latte in this city<br/>
smells like the only fruit in the world<br/>
we carve faces from. Tell me<br/>
might that be something?<br/>
