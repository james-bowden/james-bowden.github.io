## Serenity Prayer
#### James Bowden

I am of very little faith: have faith.<br/>
I feel too in control, and thus am bored: yet this is but evidence that I am out of control,<br/>
that life’s flow is out of my hands.<br/>
My work makes me feel tired and miserable: toil because you *must*, but do not work.<br/>
I yearn for that which I do not have, and cannot have: look around you, and embrace the<br/>
empty space you perceive. Therein all lies.<br/>
I find myself contemptuous, the more familiar I become: this is but a pattern, and there are always<br/>
exceptions. In truth, we live for exceptions.<br/>
Pasadena is hot as balls.<br/>
—<br/>
Sit in the rain. Feel the tears you cannot yourself render.<br/>
