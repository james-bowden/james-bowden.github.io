## *I Thought on His Desire for Three Days*
#### Linda Gregg

I chose this man, consciously, deliberately.<br/>
I thought on his desire for three days<br/>
and then said yes. In return, it was summer.<br/>
We lay on the grass in the dark and he placed<br/>
his hand on my stomach while the others<br/>
sang quietly. It was prodigious to know<br/>
his eagerness. It made me smile calmly.<br/>
That was the merging of opposite powers.<br/>
He followed me everywhere, from room to room.<br/>
Every single thing was joyous: storms, meals,<br/>
the story about the face that was the world.<br/>
There was the sound of Chicago buses stopping<br/>
near my house according to winter, summer,<br/>
raining. Shadows moved over the floor<br/>
as the sun went across the sky. I was a secret<br/>
there because you were married. I am here<br/>
to tell you I did not mind. Existence<br/>
was more valuable than that. When I was<br/>
a very young woman, I wrote: *A new spirit/<br/>
I have a new spirit/I made it myself/I dance<br/>
now alone before the mirror/There is a flower.<br/>
The leaves are a little sad/No light comes<br/>
out of the black part/with its five purple<br/>
dots of color/near the center/Oh, my dead thing/<br/>
I have a new spirit/I made it myself.* In Chicago,<br/>
a police siren ran through my heart even though<br/>
it was not for me. I was strong. I knew where<br/>
I was. I knew what I had achieved. When the wife<br/>
called and said I was a whore, I was quiet,<br/>
but inside I said, "perhaps." It has been raining<br/>
all night. Summer rain. The liveliness of it keeps<br/>
me awake. I am so happy to have lived.<br/>
