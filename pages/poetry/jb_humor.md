## Humor Study
#### James Bowden

Well, let me tell you, I hate <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;that I'm writing this. I hate<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;that you might read this. And I hate <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;that I must begin in this manner. <br/>
But as all stories begin, a word: <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*Indulge me.<br/>*
	
This word recurs in my oral corpus. I do not<br/>
like that it does. I return to it each time I try<br/>
to describe what made me feel most loved<br/>
by those I loved most. Note--separate from<br/>
what of each I appreciated. But in any sort<br/>
of relationship, this question: what am I being<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;gifted,<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;provided,<br/>
beyond their existence? Beyond that line that <br/>
is them and me me. This is what it sounds like:<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*Oh, R humored me like no one ever had<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;before. Can you believe--she read all 500<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;spewy posts on my intimates instagram?<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;And M--he humored me first, before I <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;even knew the word, before I realized<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;there existed a world in which I was not.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;And then A, who humored my poetic &<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;literary and emo spew for a while, & E,<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;who still does but doesn't feel quite<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the same. <br/>*
& on & on & on & on, ad infinitum.<br/>

And so, humor. What do we do with it, now <br/>
that we've a definition? I mean, what I want<br/>
is a collaborative life living effort. Need we be<br/>
so alone? I know solo is default, but is it so<br/>
harmful that we lean? I am trying to learn to <br/>
be weak, to withhold my defenses and love,<br/>
but I am met with hurt. That me, my roiling<br/>
complexities and foibles cannot or will not,<br/>
does it really matter which?, be held. That<br/>
I may be held so long as I am simple and<br/>
recognizable and not too unlike the rest. I<br/>
am not.<br/>

This is tangential, but I keep thinking lately of how I'm learning new ways<br/>
of saying things, not the words themselves and their definitions, but the<br/>
how, that fickle slippery little motherfucker. All of this (*do humor me*<br/>
because I had the impulse to start this off with *just to start this off, this<br/>
isn't the start of anything*, a way of cautious sharing you gifted me. &<br/>
back to where we were again, *humor me*,<br/>
but my friend told me about how she dyes<br/>
her mother's hair to beat back the greys so<br/>
that she might seem younger and be deemed<br/>
more eligible for tenure, and how every girl<br/>
who's loved me fussed over the white hairs<br/>
blossoming from my skull with such affection,<br/>
as if their diligence alone could keep me young,<br/>
and I'm sorry to report that I've some 100 odd<br/>
fellows visible just from the top of my head these<br/>
days, not to mention the multitudes that likely<br/>
lay buried beneath. It's been so long<br/>
since last I was fussed over.<br/>

And I'm trying to maintain a form here, put<br/>
this outburst into something digestible and<br/>
constructive so that you may pick it up, so <br/>
that you will be more easily convinced of its<br/>
worth, or really that the cost : benefit ratio<br/>
is low, that through my structuring you will<br/>
bear less of the messy effort that is this body,<br/>
this being, this brain. And I'm terrified to be too<br/>
much, even a little bit, did you know that I tried<br/>
to explain to my dad how hurt I've been by him<br/>
and after an hour of me doing my best to bridge<br/>
the gaping gap between us with my little rope<br/>
of words, he looked at me, distancing and unfamiliar,<br/>
and asked how I ever expected anyone to love me if it required this damn much effort and words and communication and god damn I never asked for any of this, I, like you, was born unconscious and <br/>
happen to be here and yes it's to do with me but I NEVER ASKED FOR THIS and the least you, or <br/>
anyone could do, is just try, try a little bit harder,<br/>
to understand, to see, to love. And I know, this<br/>
all sounds quite taxing and unreasonable, but<br/>
isn't this the cost of living and knowing anyone?<br/>
I've gotten high off my ass, stuffed myself full<br/>
of snacks and watched shitty movies for three<br/>
nights in a row now because this is the only way<br/>
I know to connect with my roommate on his<br/>
terms. Fell asleep thrice on the floor, trying<br/>
my best to humor him, to love him in what<br/>
ways he admits. And this, even though, or perhaps<br/>
because, I tried to help him understand where<br/>
our relationship was failing to meet my needs,<br/>
and he looked at me like my father, hands up,<br/>
confused, for what, for why, must we be so<br/>
intentional about living and loving? And I don't<br/>
know, I genuinely don't, but I've spent so long<br/>
being unloved, no loved and hurt in the same breath,<br/>
that I can no longer bear to be treated like the <br/>
wind and frankly,<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I'd rather not toss you to the wayside without even trying.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I'd rather love you. I'd rather not<br/>
let go.<br/>

All<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I know<br/>
is I'm terrified<br/>
and that I can't<br/>
be the only one.<br/>

<hr>

Angriest poem I've written in a while, perhaps ever? Anger fades out though and love returns, or I should say, is always sitting, waiting at the bottom.
