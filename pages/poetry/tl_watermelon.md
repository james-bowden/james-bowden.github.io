## For Those Who Do Not Dream
#### Timothy Liu

Watermelon seeds in the belly of a woman<br/>
four thousand years old. What survives.<br/>
Her apparent hunger. My father said<br/>
that men dream best when they go to bed<br/>
without food. Early this morning, you<br/>
called, and we ate each other to the rind.<br/>
