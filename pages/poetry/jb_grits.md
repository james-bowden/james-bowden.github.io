## empathetic grits
#### James Bowden

I met a girl on tinder the other day. she's about to be a senior at princeton. told her I probably wasn't looking<br/>
for anything, I was still reeling from Kristine and besides I wasn't that attracted to her. her name was Agnes,<br/>
and reminded me 1) of the restaurant in Pasadena Antonia had raved and even posted about, which was quite<br/>
expensive from what she told me, that I intended to try when I went back, and 2) the character from *David<br/>
Copperfield* who takes on an almost angelic role in the narrator's life, putting him and her father before herself,<br/>
who somehow ends up quite deprived herself.<br/>

we got boba and matcha at junbi, and talked and walked for a couple hours before getting wawa and talking<br/>
some more. I ate two sandwiches, I hadn't eaten anything but group meeting lunch. <br/>
she told me that she knew she was just here to fill a quota. talked about her friend from Missouri, dirt-poor but<br/>
somehow lucky enough to get into Princeton, who intended to major in film until she got here and realized<br/>
there wasn't a film major so studied German. nobody stopped her. each summer, and now that she'd<br/>
graduated, she went home and worked at the same gas station. had an uneducated boyfriend she was trying<br/>
to convince to come with her to Germany to work whatever odd jobs, and I don't think he even speaks German. why would he?<br/>

told me she genuinely wanted to go to grad school, but had to find an industry job to get her baby sister out of<br/>
her parents' toxic household in the Philippines. that she sent money to her younger sister at Dartmouth<br/>
whenever she wanted to buy supplies to cook herself nice things. how she whispered to her now-dead<br/>
grandfather in some Chinese dialect I'd never heard of, was the only of her sisters who could speak it because<br/>
she'd been his favorite, how she had to translate for them when grandma called. that she's afraid to spend<br/>
money. that most days she just eats grits. and she spent $6 on boba with me, and another $5 for a sandwich<br/>
later. what the fuck?

it got dark. I told her I had to go sleep soon, in order to visit my friend in Philly the next morning. completely<br/>
unnecessary. I mean, come on, grits. almost wanted to invite her in for sex out of empathy.<br/>

I just heard a piece called "Eating Greens" by Steven Mackey yesterday, which I think helped form the title. I also just saw the movie version of *Where the Crawdads Sing*, which was actually quite nice in my opinion, but which also contained scenes of the little girl trying to scrape together to afford some grits. This poem was written a few weeks before that, but, well, I'm adding it to the website now.
