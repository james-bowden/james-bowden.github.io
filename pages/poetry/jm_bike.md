## The Secret
#### Jeffrey McDaniel

When you were sleeping on the sofa,<br/>
I put my ear to your ear and listened<br/>
to the echo of your dreams.<br/><br/>

That is the ocean I want to dive in, merge<br/>
with the bright fish, plankton and pirate ships.<br/><br/>

I walk up to people on the street<br/>
that kind of look like you and ask them<br/>
the questions I would ask you.<br/><br/>

Can we sit on a rooftop and watch stars<br/>
dissolve into smoke rising from a chimney?<br/><br/>

Can I swing like Tarzan<br/>
in the jungle of your breathing?<br/><br/>

I don’t wish I was in your arms.<br/>
I just wish I was pedaling a bicycle<br/>
toward your arms.<br/><br/>

<hr>

This probably subconsciously influenced the writing of one of my own poems, ["Purple Starburst"](https://james-bowden.github.io/pages/poetry/jb_starburst), in retrospect.
