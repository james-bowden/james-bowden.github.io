## To My Wife on Our Anniversary
#### Jay Hopler

In Catiglione del Lago, the pines are iron-spined.<br/>
When the wind blows, they stand still & the earth<br/>
sways. If only God had forged me thus! Forced into<br/>
a stoopèd form & told to straighten up, that's as<br/>
far as His blessings ever extended in my direction.<br/>
You know what keeps me from falling apart? Luck<br/>
& duct tape. Even so, those trees have nothing on<br/>
me. Blessed as they are, all they get to hold today<br/>
is a sick man's attention &, maybe, a few birds.<br/>
