## A light store in the Bowery
#### Christian Wiman

Some love is like a light store<br/>
you slip inside only to escape<br/>
the rain. Something to see, it turns out:<br/>
the plasma lamps, mosque and lava,<br/>
the elegant icicles of the chandeliers,<br/>
shapes and shades so insistently singular<br/>
that rooms can’t help but happen around them,<br/>
lives can’t help but acquire choices and chances<br/>
inside. Some love is like an old owner<br/>
who when a child walks in with her parents<br/>
can only imagine shatterings.<br/>
And some love is like that child<br/>
asking with an earnest and exemplary awe,<br/>
“Where do they keep the dark?”<br/>
