## \[Untitled\]
#### James Bowden 

In my dreams, the father is largely absent and I take the baby from his mother’s arms, and rock him, rock him ever so carefully, I rock him, I keep rocking him, the dream never ends, I simply fall asleep.<br/> <br/>

See also ["Metempsychotic Diary: Watching My Baby Tapes"](https://james-bowden.github.io/pages/poetry/jb_baby), from which this is the ending.
