## Jesus Suckles
#### Anne Sexton

Mary, your great<br/>
white apples make me glad.<br/>
I feel your heart work its<br/>
machine and I doze like a fly.<br/>
I cough like a bird on its worm.<br/>
I'm a jelly-baby and you're my wife.<br/>
You're a rock and I the fringy algae.<br/>
You're a lily and I'm the bee that gets inside.<br/>
I close my eyes and suck you in like a fire.<br/>
I grow. I grow. I'm fattening out.<br/>
I'm a kid in a rowboat and you're the sea,<br/>
the salt, you're every fish of importance.<br/>

No. No.<br/>
All lies.<br/>
I am small <br/>
and you hold me.<br/>
You give me milk<br/>
and we are the same<br/>
and I am glad.<br/>

No. No.<br/>
All lies.<br/>
I am a truck. I run everything.<br/>
I own you.<br/>
