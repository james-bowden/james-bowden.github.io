## Narcissist: Filial Origins
#### James Bowden

1.<br/>
It occurs to me that much of embarrassment is just<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;misplaced<br/>
importance of the self.<br/><br/><br/>

2.<br/>
How to counter embarrassment:<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;who cares?<br/>
How to counter narcissism:<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;who cares?<br/><br/><br/>

3.
I am not sure how it has happened, but<br/>
the older I get, the more self-conscious <br/>
and anxious I am become.<br/>
The less I wish to be perceived<br/> 
by <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;anybody <br/>
at all.<br/>

So it turns out that many strong emotions have roots<br/>
in narcissism. Excitement--prospects for self. Love--<br/>
reciprocal, recursive excitement with an other. Fear--love<br/>
of the body, this small existence. Anxiety--fear of <br/>
wrongly placed judgement. Self-consciousness--anxiety,<br/>
and make it introspective. Embarrassment--self-consciousness<br/>
at our actions betraying the self we are fighting to construct.<br/>
And so on, and so forth. And how impeccably positive <br/>
makes space for negative. Defines it, really.<br/><br/><br/>

4.<br/>
My father scares me. I scare me. In me, his narcissism<br/>
manifested as lack of agency, I, forever an extension<br/>
of his untrusting arm. Beneath the surface, I see him:<br/>
naked and lonely and afraid. Maybe you are like me.<br/>
Maybe I am like you. He comes to fear my words,<br/>
piercing too deep. What is insecurity, bottled for<br/>
62 years? What becomes of our fermentation?<br/>
I try to distill myself, but taste only bitter liquor.<br/>
I try to find words for us, a bridge, a home, <br/>
a door. One must imagine Telemachus<br/>
grasping.<br/><br/><br/>

5.<br/>
I am my father, only modern.<br/><br/><br/>

<hr>

Draws *maybe you are like me* bit from Olena Kalytiak Davis' ["may be you are like me: scared and awake"](http://www.lit-hum.org/2014/08/may-be-you-are-like-me-scared-and-awake.html?m=1). 
The title comes from *Batman: Arkham Origins*. Telemachus fused with that Sisyphus line I've been splattering everywhere, at the end.
