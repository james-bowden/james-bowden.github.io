## And Then God Said, *Let There Be Rain*
#### James Bowden

You mean that were it raining this quiet Sunday morning, <br/>
you would untangle your legs from mine to sit apew? I, <br/>
not for the world, not for all that is holy. If He won't <br/>
absolve us these fleeting minutes where we <br/>
want nothing beyond an arm's reach, <br/>
I shall. He truly is a needy <br/>
lover. Your soft, sleepy <br/>
smile makes one <br/>
of me, too.<br/>
