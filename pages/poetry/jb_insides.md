## Finally, my insides
#### James Bowden

Remember the night we watched *Inside Out*?<br/>
I was a small bird, scared, shaken. You held me<br/>
in your cupped palms and spoke softly to me.<br/>
Kissed my forehead. Let me disappear into the<br/>
corner of the walls, your twin bed. I didn’t know<br/>
how to cry then, but if I had, I would have. I didn’t<br/>
know how to love then, but I did. When morning came,<br/>
you didn’t know what to do with me and I didn’t<br/>
want to leave the peace, your embrace. A flash <br/>
of something I couldn’t yet place, didn’t yet understand.<br/>
If I could now, I would have stopped running,<br/>
right there, in that moment, with you. <br/>
From hundreds of miles upstate, I remember <br/>
and I am torn to pieces but it is something and I smile.<br/>
Past sweetness *is* suggestion that still<br/>
more will come, that I will not live in the cold for ever.<br/>
Forgive me, not knowing how to surrender.<br/>
I grow tired now and surrender becomes me. By<br/>
which I mean to say, *I’m ready*. <br/>

<hr>

Sad. Not a great poem but a record of emotions on this here day. In my head are a few things: this line from "Holocene", *and that you played me Lip Parade*, which for whatever reason, feels like an equivalent experience. 
And this song, [Phoenix](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OhT7Tophch4), by Big Red Machine (also a Justin Vernon band TM), which describes how I'm feeling decently.
