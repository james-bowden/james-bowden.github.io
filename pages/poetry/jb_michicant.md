## *I was in first-grade, I was a boy, I was a tender age*
####  James Bowden

*(title to be read in cadence of first lyric of “Michicant”)* <br/>

For whatever reason, I identify more with the thing<br/>
being chosen than the chooser. More the object<br/>
than the user.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I’m taking a deep<br/>
breath in, I’m inhaling everything, and when<br/>
I’m ready, I’ll let out the slightest of sighs:<br/>
To Topple Dominoes.<br/>
