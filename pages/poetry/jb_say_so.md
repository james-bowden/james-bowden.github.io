## why didn't u just say so
#### James Bowden

Everything seems to continue coming back<br/>
full circle, as if there are fundamental truths<br/>
with either the world or with me, that require<br/>
life to play out similarly *ad infinitum*. Or perhaps,<br/>
*ad mutatio*. Let us see what it takes for change to occur.<br/><br/>

Case 1:<br/>
We love each other, until we don't. Or, until I don't, at least.<br/>
What happens? Your insecurities begin to play out all over me,<br/>
consume me in ways I am uncomfortable with. You use me and<br/>
expect that I am okay with it. I am not. Why am I unable to treat<br/>
your hurt, support you? You present things I am trying to wash<br/>
myself of, you induce fear where I am trying to be calm, really<br/>
I should love you but I am hurt and I cannot and so I do not.<br/>

Repeat after me: *you're just fucking like me!*<br/>
Forgive me: *I did not know myself then*.<br/><br/>

Case 2:<br/>
We love each other, until I must leave. This doesn't stop the<br/>
loving, as everyone knows, but distance. We love from a distance.<br/>
You burn me when that distance becomes too small. As I boil, I think<br/>
of you first--what bubbles up in me, both answer to our conflict and an<br/>
irrepressible truth you must know. More than that, you are she who<br/>
I feel most comfortable breaking before, and so I do. My fear, my<br/>
insecurities, my admissions of smallness: and you love me for it,<br/>
you do, and I have loved you for our sameness from the start,<br/>
only I did not allow you to See me, much less Help me then,<br/>
and you leave me for it too, no I leave you?, no we both do<br/>
and are sad and now you won't talk to me. You live on,<br/>
a series of sixty screenshots in my favorites folder.<br/>

I repeat after you: *you're just fucking like me!*<br/>
Forgive me: *I did not know my smallness then*.<br/><br/>

Case 3:<br/>
We love each other, until you don't. Or maybe you do,<br/>
but you live across the country now and our letters sour<br/>
and you no longer facetime me nor respond to my texts. I<br/>
wonder if I have become too much for you to carry. I wonder<br/>
if you're tired of my pining. Though I haven't to wonder for long;<br/>
for you tell me this explicitly, on paper! Me, a self-absorbed narcissist,<br/>
and you, fine and peachy and unbothered and certainly not empathetic<br/>
enough to be gentle, constructive, to try to See and Help. I am all anger<br/>
and broken heartbeats, though not for so long. You send me your blog.<br/>
And right before my eyes: your own gross admissions of self-centered<br/>
bloat, dear god, ~~why~~ I mean no fucking wonder you respond to me<br/>
with meanness, holy shit, you evil stepmother you bitch of glass,<br/>
and how can I explain anything to you with words where only a<br/>
hug or violence will suffice? Every person I hurt, I am. Every<br/>
person I am hurt by, I am.<br/>

I'm growing tired of repeating, but *you're just fucking like me*,<br/>
I forgive you I forgive you I forgive you I forgive me and let us<br/>

just move Forward and Love.

<hr>

This was an odd poem. It references three people that I have loved and may have actually helped distance me from the third one. For shame. In short, where I meant
my poetry to be reflection and vulnerability, it was taken for certainty and judgement and no such thing was meant. I admit that the wording was raw
and unpolished and that I am at fault for that. But, at a certain point, just have to keep a dialogue open.
