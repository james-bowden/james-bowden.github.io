## Purple starburst
#### James Bowden

I mean, look -- I'm over her. I've<br/>
no<br/>
intentions nor desires for her, no<br/>
hope,<br/>
but somehow every, and I mean<br/>
every,<br/>
time I see a small Asian woman in the<br/>
distance<br/>
with a ~~too-wide~~ face and carefree<br/>
but<br/>
elegant stature, almost sway-y, really<br/>
my brain<br/>
(including the two consecutive days on which it really was <br/>
her)<br/>
stops--*look natural! do I say something to her, do I<br/>
acknowledge<br/>
that I saw her? no, god no! what about the <br/>
guy<br/>
with her wait I mean I'm over it none of this<br/>
matters*<br/>
--if you can call it that, and my heart bursts<br/>
again<br/>

and then a minute later I continue on with my life,<br/>
perfectly fine without her in it?<br/>

*Ambivalence*, darling.
