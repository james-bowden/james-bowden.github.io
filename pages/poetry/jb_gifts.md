## Little drummer boy
#### James Bowden

Here I am at your door, humbling<br/>
myself. With a gift of watermelon<br/>
pickle, or popcorn and fruit snacks,<br/>
or a man’s soul bound in paper. I’ll<br/>
take off my shoes, leave my ego at<br/>
the door, and bare my soul, too--<br/>
would you like a hug? I scoffed<br/>
when I read the part about the<br/>
lady’s horses dying, because so<br/>
much else was dying around<br/>
Ocean. I am sorry. We are all<br/>
penetrable.<br/>

<hr>

This, in reference to Ocean Vuong's *On Earth We're Briefly Gorgeous* in paper and in scene, as well as a funnily-titled book of poems targeting children called *A Gift of Watermelon Pickle*. Pa - rum pum pum pum.
