## Needfulness Study
#### James Bowden

All those things you thought you were choosing to do? Yeah, that’s right, the hobbies the people the pining the thinking even. Yes, the thinking itself. The thinking you thought of as you yourself. Turns out they were all needful. That you were bound. Even in your agency, exertion of power—your weakness is front and center. Why all of that power and turned inward? With great use of power, great need is implicated. If you are not in danger of being hurt—if you are loved without condition—if you trust yourself—if you are of little need—why is your power even relevant? It is not. It is not needed.
