## Mr. Sandman brings me a dream
#### James Bowden 

*in memory of Rachel Sun*<br/><br/>


No actually, many dreams, more than I asked for!<br/>
I close my eyes <br/>
and darkness falls <br/>
upon the face of the deep.<br/>
Then, from it, a blip: a single ship.<br/>

I’m in some sort of class once again.<br/>
I’m being pick-me, once again.<br/>
A friend who’s more of an acquaintance<br/>
by now is sitting beside me, and I don’t know<br/>
what’s going on. I don’t know<br/>
why I am here. But I forget and soon enough<br/>
am flirting with the girl on my right, mercilessly,<br/>
and none of it means anything to me. I give this dream <br/>
a 3-star rating and narrow my metaphorical eyes at Mr. Sandman.<br/>

And so eventually, the clime changes. I’m no longer<br/>
in class, and the friends around me are no longer my<br/>
age. All of the older students I loved in college. And<br/>
a kiddie pool of sorts. And it’s fun and all and I forget<br/>
myself long enough to sink in to my old behaviors,<br/>
banter with the boys and splash sillily around the pool.<br/>
I find myself lounging in a chair when<br/>

Rachel shows up. Halfway through. And I’m not at<br/>
all prepared for it, though maybe I should be, given<br/>
her recent resurgence in my dreams. I sit and try to<br/>
pretend everything is normal and it kind of is. I should<br/>
specify which kind of normal it is: the kind wherein she<br/>
does not come over to the chair and giggle with me, but<br/>
smiles from afar. Seems to pay me little more mind than<br/>
anyone else around us. And I’m not damaged, *I’m not*, but<br/>
I find myself needing a breather and so around the block I<br/>
disappear. In this normal in which attention is not paid me<br/>
and I am not allowed to pay it her, I feel unsettled. I am<br/>
excluded from the bubble I want to be a part of. Namely,<br/>
that of her desire. Namely, that of her affection. I cannot deal.<br/>

And eventually I’m back and in the lounge chair and back<br/>
to the normal I’ve been granted. I’m not sure I’ve ever been<br/>
allowed a Rachel dream where we are still together. Perhaps<br/>
my brain rebels so strongly against this craved untruth that<br/>
stability becomes impossible, even for a dream-minute. And<br/>
I’ve never been lucid before, either: in dream or in real-life with her.<br/>
And everything is going normal still except my thoughts are a mess<br/>
particles everywhere a radioactive mess not unlike real life to*day*<br/>
and at the end she walks up to me to ask if I am okay: I’ve been twitching<br/>
every time she says my name or refers to me, and it’s getting awkward<br/>
because everyone else is noticing it too. Fractures are beginning to <br/>
appear in the mock-air and I want to tell her something true. But<br/>
dream-me doesn’t know what to say. He: nothing more than a limp<br/>
avatar of my hidden desires and muted agency. It’s a wonder<br/>

I wake up though, and the sun is rising over Chicago. Orange on<br/>
the building skyline. What more is there to do? What can I ever <br/>
say?<br/><br/>
