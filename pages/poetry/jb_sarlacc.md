## Self-Portrait as a Sarlacc
#### James Bowden and Eve Blank

The door is lighter than expected. I think, *I must be going soon*, but it stops raining.<br/>
Perhaps I can stay awhile. Things quickly go from slow to fast. Your chin on my shoulder,<br/>
now my fingers traipse your stomach. I’ve always thought that being wine-drunk has a bit<br/>
of a different quality to it, though only when I’m not blacked barfing my bed burgundy. <br/>
*Sorry, was that too serious, I didn’t mean to kill the mood*. Fear not, young padawan:<br/>
nothing can kill a hunger this yawning. Not even the first part of a Netflix docu-series <br/>
on Mormons, a senile man with 30 wives and acquiring younger ones all the time.<br/>
Not even *Twilight*. <br/>

Trust me: the first time I made love to this girl, we’d just finished watching *Inception*<br/>
plus a creepy *Black Mirror* episode it reminded her of. The guy died. At least in the former<br/>
you get a Schrodinger’s Leo Dicaprio of sorts, not just skull full of mush. We were getting<br/>
all existentialist, no, more nihilist but scared of the dark, *well, uh, I know the last four hours<br/>
have been anything but aphrodisiac, but well, we’re sitting on my bed and I’m quite attracted to you.*<br/>
~~We fucked each other’s brains out.~~ We had the most awkward sex of my life, don’t get me wrong it was good,<br/>
*what do you want to do next?*, \*giggle\*, *I don’t know, have sex with you*, \*giggle\* \*turns away\*, you get the point,<br/>
for 30 minutes I was incapable of doubting the authenticity of this world,<br/>
all nails sweaty skin tongue teeth and poorly stifled laughter.<br/>

I still remember the distinct shape of her body. It’s been thousands of years.<br/>
Most days, I’m waiting to fall backward into reality again. *Non, je ne regrette rien* never plays.<br/>
You give me nothing but a nasty case of acid reflux.<br/>

Another in our collaborative poetry series! This takes from [the sarlacc in Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarlacc), as well as the [*Inception*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inception) in both its concept, the "kick" scene and the track that signals it, [*Non, je ne regrette rien*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non,_je_ne_regrette_rien). A good name for a song too in both that and this context.
