## Poem Number 99927364832 on the No Free Lunch Theorem, or Don’t Bother Trying to Optimize, Overall Nothing Beats Random
#### James Bowden

It turns out<br/>
The Library of Babel<br/>
had it right <br/>
all along.<br/>
Xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx, ad infinitum, or to the 410 page mark.<br/>

<hr>

This series of poems was generally inspired in form by June Jordan's short poem, ["Poem Number Two on Bell’s Theorem, or The New Physicality of Long Distance Love"](https://poets.org/poem/poem-number-two-bells-theorem-or-new-physicality-long-distance-love).<br/>
