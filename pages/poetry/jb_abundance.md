## abundance
#### James Bowden

*after Amy Schmidt*<br/>

somehow, the world does not feel like it has enough.<br/>
how silly. there is more than enough. always has been.<br/>
you just have to look around you and breathe in.<br/>
it breaks me, how much enough people do not see.<br/>
it's there. it exists. you are fine.<br/>
you will be fine. be gentle and soft<br/>
with others. <br/>
this is my version of good bones.<br/>

Draws from ["Abundance"](https://www.amywoschekschmidt.com/poetry/abundance) and ["Good Bones"](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/89897/good-bones).
