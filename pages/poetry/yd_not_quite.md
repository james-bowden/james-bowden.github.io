## the not quite love
#### Yrsa Daley-Ward

I haven't been home in nearly two weeks.<br/>
My new lover has a fridge full of beer<br/>
and can almost make jollof rice<br/>
also the sex is good<br/>
and we are falling into someting we<br/>
will soon mistake for love<br/>

anyway,<br/>
'home' is a problem. There are the<br/>
bills and there<br/>
are the mice<br/>
plus<br/>
there is that feeling you get<br/>
when you catch up with yourself.<br/>
