## Elegy III (Texting You)
#### James Bowden

1.<br/>
It was your birthday yesterday, and I slept warm<br/>
as a button after all of our drunk banter and play.<br/>
I slept, happily, having spent seven hours carving<br/>
a gift for you--seven hours I'd have rather spent<br/>
with you--and getting to spend an hour and a ha<br/>
lf with you under the pretense of giving it. A<br/>
welcome morsel. You texted me a selfie of you<br/>
crying right after, having read my card. In it, I<br/>
wrote what I am going to try to convey here again.<br/>

2.<br/>
You have a new boyfriend. We know this. It is<br/>
nobody's fault. None of this is. My existence, <br/>
our closeness makes him uncomfortable. As it<br/>
should. You don't spend much time with me<br/>
as a result. Even though the time we spend is <br/>
wonderful. Even though I ask for nothing but<br/>
closeness. This is an old story. A decision will<br/>
be made. There is never really a decision to be<br/>
made. There is no other version of this story.<br/>
You have known my decision for a while, now.<br/>

3.<br/>
Darling:<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;this is really sad and I don't think I <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;should be telling you this now but I <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;don't want to not<br/>
Omg u always stress me out when <br/>
u say stuff like that<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;no not stress<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;but I had this wonderful dream last<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;night and idk was doing shit with<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;people whatever whatever and I<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;woke up and the only concrete<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;thing that stuck with me was that in<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the dream you were texting me <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;more frequently and of your own <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;accord and I almost went to check<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;for some google form i thought<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;you'd sent me<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;and anyhow I was quite happy bc of<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;that and then I remembered to be<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;sad eventually<br/>
:(<br/>
I'm sorry<br/>
that is sad<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;don't<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;but yeah<br/>

4.<br/>
I want<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;you to know<br/>
many things.<br/>
I want<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;you to know<br/>
that today I keep finding myself falling into<br/>
counterfactuals. Daydreaming. How things<br/>
might be. How we could make it work this<br/>
time, if we really tried. *We can make it if<br/>
we try*, Bill Withers blathers. <br/>
I want<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;you to know<br/>
how much there is I would like to share with<br/>
you that I cannot for fear of imposition. <br/>
I want<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;you to know<br/>
that I went and came, eyes closed and<br/>
probably in 30 seconds, and it was the<br/>
best I've had in quite some time. <br/>

5.<br/>
My roommate told me that we were bickering<br/>
again last night. That you yelled at me, in front<br/>
of all of your friends, that at least you'd found<br/>
a new boyfriend, and where was my new girl<br/>
friend? The answer is that: I prefer to sit with<br/>
my pain, marinate in it, know it, rather than<br/>
drag an arbiter into it. You (yes, you--)<br/>
should know that doesn't end well. I was cer<br/>
tainly too intoxicated to tell you that. Probab<br/>
ly just happy to be yelled at by you, again.<br/>

6.<br/>
I keep wondering what the right words are. I<br/>
tell you often that I love you, though we (for<br/>
the time being) understand it to be platonic.<br/>
I tell you that I miss u. There's a dream I have<br/>
in which I love the world, no, in which the <br/>
world loves me back and I feel it and I believe <br/>
it and that (that) is why it is a dream. When I <br/>
asked for space, I didn't mean like this. <br/>
Everyone knows that you can't write your <br/>
way out of grief. That grief never turns into <br/>
anything but grief, and OK, I can grieve you <br/>
forever. But I wanted you here, in the middle <br/>
of my book. Yes, a complaint about what I lost<br/>
and what it feels like to lose you. But also, you. <br/>
Your teary face. Your texts, even though dreamt,<br/>
wholly lighting up the morning until my conscious<br/>
could kick back in and realize: you've gone<br/>

7.<br/>
You already know all of this. But here:<br/>
I've organized it for you, for myself,<br/>
to relieve my mind that I may turn<br/>
to other tasks that need doing. <br/>
I did not write all this in your card<br/>
because I was not ready to. I typically<br/>
arrive about 9 months too late. At <br/>
least. You know exactly where<br/>
to find me, even if you're 9<br/>
years too late. hi<br/><br/>

<hr>

More of a therapeutic poem than a good poem. Drawing from a bunch of poems I've loved: Mikko Harvey's ["Wind-Related Ripple in the Wheatfield"](https://sixthfinch.com/harvey3.html), Jason Schneiderman's "Elegy VIII (Missing You)", Cameron Awkward-Rich's "Meditations in an Emergency", etc.
