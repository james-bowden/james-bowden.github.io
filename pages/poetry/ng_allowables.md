## Allowables
#### Nikki Giovanni

I killed a spider<br/>
Not a murderous brown recluse<br/>
Nor even a black widow<br/>
And if the truth were told this<br/>
Was only a small<br/>
Sort of papery spider<br/>
Who should have run<br/>
When I picked up the book<br/>
But she didn’t<br/>
And she scared me<br/>
And I smashed her<br/>
I don’t think<br/>
I’m allowed<br/>
To kill something<br/>
Because I am<br/>
Frightened<br/>
