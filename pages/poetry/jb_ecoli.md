## A Dirge to Exponential Growth
#### James Bowden

I once was an *E. coli*<br/>
Having a feast,<br/>
Floating through ravioli<br/>
Fending off indigestion and disease.<br/>
'Til suddenly a splitting pain in my nucleoli<br/>
Ripped through me cytoskeleton causing my cell wall to crease.<br/>
As half of my ribosomes bade me goodbye<br/>
I lamented that I'd never grow as big as a yeast.<br/>

I tried to think<br/>
But there were two of me now<br/>
So almost in sync<br/>
Down on nutrients we did chow.<br/>
But before I could blink<br/>
Another two of me I did sow<br/>
'Til my spacious precinct<br/>
Was jam packed as a powwow.<br/>

Now my sixth sense did tingle<br/>
And quorum bells did jingle<br/>
As signaling molecules did mingle<br/>
Oh, how I wished I were single!<br/>

Still our hunger intensified<br/>
And with no food in sight,<br/>
We ate up the lining and brought about our demise.<br/>
Little did we know of James' great plight<br/>
Until we were suddenly flushed out with the tide.<br/>

So imagine my relief when I awoke from my nap<br/>
Eukaryotic, multicellular, and not drowning in crap!<br/>
