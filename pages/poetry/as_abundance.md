## *Abundance*
#### Amy Schmidt 

*in memory of Mary Oliver*<br/>

It’s impossible to be lonely<br/>
when you’re zesting an orange.<br/>
Scrape the soft rind once<br/>
and the whole room<br/>
fills with fruit.<br/>
Look around: you have<br/>
more than enough.<br/>
Always have.<br/>
You just didn’t notice<br/>
until now.<br/>
