## Patchwork Penitence
#### James Bowden

So this is how adulthood is to be christened--with too many shirts, none of them nice enough,<br/>
and loneliness lurking around every corner of this empty city<br/>
with familial isolation, more differance<br/><br/><br/><br/>




and most notably<br/>
without you<br/>
(in the plural)<br/><br/>


people *paddleboard* outside my window<br/>
and I haven't the foggiest why I am here.<br/><br/>


my thoughts a postmates even though I think not of<br/>
jumble, nor pho<br/>

<hr>

So the postmates/pho/jumble line comes from this advertising campaign Postmates was doing that I kept seeing all around, where they'd say something like ["when all you can pho is think about"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ytuepu7hBXE). I realize that I definitely needed to explain this reference, since I think this same summer Postmates was acquired by Uber while I was interning at Uber.

And then "differance", I picked up from Danielewski's *House of Leaves* (fantastic experimental novel, thanks Chandrew!), which I guess means something like [difference and deferral of meaning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diff%C3%A9rance). I think it's a nice word.
