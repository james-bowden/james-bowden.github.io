## Conviction as default
#### James Bowden

I know it's not you.<br/>
And did I ever know that it was?<br/>
And must there be a you<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;for me to be me<br/>
And what is the human condition but drowning in one's own filth?<br/>

And repeating one's sins til some gracious god untethers you<br/>
