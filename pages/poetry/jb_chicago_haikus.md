## Chicago Haikus
#### James Bowden 

Smooth sky blue teapot<br/>
Max brings me a little cup<br/>
Together we sip<br/>

Linger and linger<br/>
Then off with the lights to sleep<br/>
And farewell til spring<br/><br/>
