## The world isn't ready for my love
#### James Bowden

*I've got nothing.*<br>
<p style="text-align: right;">--The Script, <i>Nothing</i><br/><br/>
 </p>

I wrote this girl I'd just met a letter. Stuck some first impressions on a Hokusai print card,<br/>
which I'd bought because the first girl I ever loved, Rachel, had a tapestry of the wave<br/>
on her wall, across from where she tickled me tender. Why a letter? <br/>
Well, the girl I most recently loved, Antonia, was quite into writing letters <br/>
and wrote me many an email, and, well, <br/>
have you ever loved someone? How could I not get into writing letters?<br/>

We're getting a little far from the original subject, Kristine. But how else do you love <br/>
someone new than in the ways you know how? No, I didn't stumble upon love by myself <br/>
or through my parents. Of course not. I'm no philosopher and they're hardly even role models.<br/>
Let's not be overly romantic here.<br/>

Well, I thought it'd be cute to look back at either way. Licked it shut, <br/>
tucked in my desk drawer. I mean, we proceeded to have sex the next day <br/>
after I'd written the letter, so that has to mean something. Started hanging out.<br/>
Starting loving her a little, missing her. Her too-wide smile, gently framed by her jawline <br/>
that's (in theory) too well-defined for a girl. The way she clasps my face between her palms. <br/>
Kisses me cutely. Rakes her nails down my back when I ask her to. I'm writing this poem <br/>
because I don't know what the fuck she does when she's not with me but it's<br/>
almost never respond to my texts, she kept mentioning boundaries, <br/>
seemed scared to fall in, said she should not sleep over <br/>
on weeknights because it made her not<br/>
want to get out of my bed <br/>
in the morning.<br/>

The point is, I've written all three of these girls love letters. Never at the "opportune"<br/>
time, but then again, I never expected anything to come of them. I mean, sure, <br/>
I wanted them like you'd want anyone you love. But these love letters,<br/>
they weren't rhetorical. Just artifacts of love. Expressions of my feel-<br/>
ings. Love regardless of outcome, response. Selfless beyond sensibility.<br/>
As unconditional as I know how to be.<br/>

Neither of the first two were received very well. I'm uncertain I'll get to mail the third<br/>
at this rate.<br/>
