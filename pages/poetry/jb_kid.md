## [Term Ends] At the California Institute of Technology
#### James Bowden

The beckoning, the belonging; it really is lovely you know. The fact that we’re owed nothing but still so much to be wanted and so much already had. Summer! Moonlight! Cool breezes and forever in my hands and sleep. Cut fruit and veggies. I am excited and life is beautiful once more—I kid; it always has been, especially from just outside the fray. I kid—kid energy—*weeeeeeeeeeee!*<br/>
