## Salty Linings Playbook
#### James Bowden

My legs are shaky. It seems to stem from the calves. By now, I know<br/>
better. Unsteadiness begins in the foundation beneath my feet.<br/>

Observation: tears sting my face skin. I allow them to dry there: a relic. <br/>
It is tomorrow afternoon now, and still I have not washed of them.<br/>

I do not want to believe that he is incapable. I do not want to accept <br/>
his faults as pathological. The only father we have. To leave him for lost,<br/>

to leave us lost, too.<br/>

---<br/>

My brothers bike circles about me while I walk, new cupcake maker in <br/>
hand. To be alone and together. Little ones: I am going to bake you a cake, <br/>
no, several cups of cakes, where love comes only as sugar. Where intent<br/>
turns to sweetness directly. No debt looming combustible over our meek<br/>
heads. Just sugar. Just sugar, and bits of salt cracking off my cheeks.<br/><br/>
