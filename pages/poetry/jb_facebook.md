## My Father and I are no longer friends on Facebook
#### James Bowden


1.<br/>
I, James Christopher Kapono Bowden, was born on March 28th, 2001 to two expectant parents.<br/>
Two excited parents. I was to be a Jennifer, like my miscarried forebear. I am male, ergo James.<br/><br/>

2.<br/>
Someone must give. This is a very old story.<br/>
There is no other version of this story.<br/><br/>

3.<br/>
My father wonders what happened. Turns over all these pictures, me, small, smiling.<br/>
*No father has even been so holy.* <br/><br/>

4.<br/>
I give, give, give until I can no longer see a self. What child<br/>
has ever been so holy?<br/><br/>

5.<br/>
*No child has ever been so self-centered.* In the car one morning, my father tells me to look up<br/>
the words "belligerent" and "willful". I do that evening. <br/>
The next morning, I'm back out on the sidewalk halfway to school,<br/>
nose kissing concrete in giving. To give is to need.<br/><br/>

6.<br/>
We used to watch *The Lion King* over and over. He, of course,<br/>
is Mufasa. These days, he tells me I am Scar.<br/>
*That's okay*, he laughs. *One of your brothers will be Simba.*<br/><br/>

7.<br/>
I can no longer cry. I try, and sputter.<br/>
I realize that I've never been upset in my father's presence without him raising me fifteenfold.<br/>
People I love leave. Rachel notes that I didn't tell my parents about her until I had to.<br/>
I break.<br/>
It takes some time, but emotions come to be more than some abstract notion.<br/>
I relearn how to sob. I break again.<br/><br/>

8.<br/>
What good is giving when it's expected? Define *taking*.<br/>
My father shoves a large number in front of me. I pay.<br/>
I ask about the state of our family finances. I ask <br/>
how urgent. I ask<br/>
if I need to get a job for my brothers' tuition, for my parents' retirement, medical bills.<br/>
I offer to drop self. I ask <br/>
for numbers. I'll need to know this soon anyway, you not around forever and all.<br/>
*God forbid you touch my will--you think I'd trust you to do things the way I want them done?*<br/>
To ask, to give.<br/><br/>

9.<br/>
All I feel anymore: fear of abandonment. To ease, submit. Is an option<br/>
really an option when one cannot choose it? I, like a recalcitrant puppy.<br/>
I want for nothing but resolution. Once more, I offer myself. I give.<br/><br/>

10.<br/>
I am moved out, but<br/>
wish my father would see how he turns all he touches to hurt, my<br/>
mother brother brother. I try reason. I try emotion. He tells me to <br/>
inscribe my grievances upon his gravestone, just leave him alone.<br/>
*I unfriended you on Facebook so you wouldn't see my posts and have something<br/>
negative to say about them. Did you even notice? Did you?* No father has ever been<br/>
so<br/>
small.<br/><br/>


11.<br/>
I--<br/>
My arms are tired. I fend back the world in all its coldness.<br/>
Who will stand behind me? <br/>
I, the last line.<br/><br/>
