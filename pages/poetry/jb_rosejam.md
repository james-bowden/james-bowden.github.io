## Rose Jam
#### James Bowden

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;HANDMADE <br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;BY GLADICE<br/>
ON 21-SEP-21, as I <br/>
made by you. $10 <br/>
is a lot for a tiny b-<br/>
ottle of shower gel<br/>
but hardly anything <br/>
to smell of you, re-<br/>
minisce of you, be<br/>
inoculated by you <br/>
at least once more.<br/>

<hr>

I'm told this is called a "concrete poem" in its shape.
