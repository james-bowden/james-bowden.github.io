## Insanity
#### James Bowden

Over and over and again and again <br/>
Could it be true that I’m just static then? <br/>
No. I cannot subscribe to that <br/>
Or it shall be true. <br/>

Still believing that something will change <br/>
It’ll be better next time because of the pangs <br/>
Funny. I wish I could. <br/>
Maybe it’d be true. <br/>

Perhaps I am simply as dull and broken <br/>
As what these ugly fingernails have spoken <br/>
They are cancerous <br/>
Stop growing and I’ll heal. <br/>

But this world does not pander to me <br/>
So I try again, as positive as can be <br/>
And again and again <br/>
Until you relieve me from this mundane burden <br/>
That perhaps, I must carry simply because <br/>
I cannot imagine <br/>
That I could be as strong <br/>
As me. <br/>
