## JUST THE WIND FOR A SOUND, SOFTLY
#### Carl Phillips


There’s a weed whose name I’ve meant all summer<br/>
to find out: in the heat of the day, dangling pods hardly<br/>
worth noticing; in the night, blue flowers ... It’s as if<br/>
a side of me that he’d forgotten had forced into the light,<br/>
briefly, a side of him I’d never seen before, and now<br/>
I’ve seen it. It is hard to see anyone who has become<br/>
like your own body to you. And now I can't forget. <br/>
