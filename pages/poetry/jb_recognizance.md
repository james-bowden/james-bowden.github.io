## Recognizance Prayer
#### James Bowden

How subtly twilight slips past me, how awake I am in its wake. How long the night is, yet how easily it melts into day and back again and I no wiser. Where is the time to process, ever? Must I only see once the blackness sets in? Help me to detect presence rather than absence, and thus live a series of oases and not one of droughts.
