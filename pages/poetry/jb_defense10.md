## defense mechanism #10
#### James Bowden

Life continues on as if it had never stopped, as if it never were *different*, <br/>
and I continue to adjust. And by adjust, I mean call upon the same old <br/>
strategies for filling holes and feeling safe without your fingers wrapped <br/>
sweatily through mine. <br/>

Would you have guessed that Livermore would be so beautiful? <br/>
You’d not have been as surprised as I am. The peaceful plaza, <br/>
the vines across the fountains, the cool air calm against the small-<br/>
town buzz of the establishments. <br/>

The sky such a clear blue. The dead fields such a lovable yellow. Dry. <br/>
I can’t escape the feeling that I could be content, free even, here. <br/><br/>


When the plane shudders and begins to roll, I ask the girl beside me to open the window with only mild <br/>
thought of how my actions might reflect (negatively) (advantageously) upon me. Oakland sprawls out <br/>
beneath us. I find her cute, unconventionally so, something about her voice and mannerisms, and find <br/>
myself cathecting upon her. I want to look outside, I want to look at her, I want to look at everything; <br/>
really I don’t care and just want to feel free to do any of these things and so I do them to convince myself<br/> 
that I am indeed free and so I do and so I am. *You cannot hurt me: I am an impenetrable fortress of* <br/>
*observation. I am curious as the clouds.* I soon grow tired of peering around the plane wistfully <br/>
(deliberately) and thinking of how everyone else might be perceiving me. Dear god. I read bell hooks and <br/>
try to be loving toward myself. I vow to make a list of positive affirmations, soon, though I must admit that<br/> 
the work I should do weighs more heavily upon my mind. I read Brooke Horvath. When the plane lands in <br/>
LA, some people at the front clap. It's Southwest though, and really I should have expected it. Did you <br/>
know that to imagine something infinite, you can simply visualize a finite version and say aloud, *that, but* <br/>
*infinite*? I mention this because I recently learned that spikes are just high-dimensional smooth surfaces <br/>
projected down. Think 2-d surface, clench your eyes shut, pause, *fifty!* Around me, the world tends toward<br/> 
the smooth. The sharp, the outstanding get worn down, probably not even into satisfying smoothness, <br/>
just to rough. Barring significant force to support said sharpness, or unimaginable gentleness to make the <br/>
transition less jagged for those of us not born polished. I sit in the middle seat, shoulders slightly forward, <br/>
so as not to inconvenience the two people around me, whom I might love, in any way. I close my eyes. <br/>
What else is there to say? What else is there to do?<br/>
