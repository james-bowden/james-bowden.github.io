## The Gods Watch Us Through the Window
#### Beth Ann Fennelly

We sit at the table with the fourth side open,<br/>
the perfect family show. Claire belts "Twinkle, Twinkle,"<br/>
*How I wa wa* (mumble) *are!*<br/>
We beam like stars. Isn't she gifted? Isn't life great?<br/>

What a large target we make.<br/>
The great dramas all begin like this:<br/>
a surfeit of happiness, a glass-smooth pond<br/>
just begging for a stone.<br/>

<hr>

*Just begging for a stone.* I, too.
