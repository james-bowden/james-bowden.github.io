## Lilting
#### Derrick Austin

In bed, we are lavender together.<br/>
We watch the little theater of hours.<br/>

Walking her dogs, our neighbor<br/>
Crosses the lakeside corner,<br/>

Hoop earrings<br/>
Echoing the birch's colors.<br/>

*Tend your joy*, you whisper,<br/>
As if charm against eviction or some harm<br/>

We might inflict on each other.<br/>
For once, I don't hear you<br/>

From the room called<br/>
Memory. Open the window.<br/>

Risk, breath, our seasons,<br/>
Let them in. Let them in.<br/>
