## Whittling Haiku
#### James Bowden

**v2**

I got a little<br/>
woodcarving kit, now I'd like <br/>
to whittle some shit!!<br/><br/>

**v1**

I got a little<br/>
woodcarving kit, and now I'd<br/>
like to carve some shit!!<br/><br/>


<hr>

Versions because I've been toying with the rhyming in my head a little bit--which sounds better to you?

Adding to the rhymery in my head, the badge in cub/boy scouts that allows you to wield a pocketknife is called the whittling chip / totin' chip, hence shit.
