## Whittling Haiku
#### James Bowden

I got a little<br/>
woodcarving kit, and now I'd<br/>
like to carve some shit!!<br/><br/>

<hr>

Adding to the rhymery in my head, the badge in cub/boy scouts that allows you to wield a pocketknife is called the whittling chip / totin' chip, hence shit.
