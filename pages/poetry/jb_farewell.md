## Farewell
#### James Bowden 

Farewell--fare thee well--where is there room for saying goodbye<br/>
when such an embrace exists?<br/>
Beauty languishes on the tip of my tongue and I've nothing<br/>
but heart for anyone.<br/>

This came about while I was watching the Lord of the Rings and Hobbit series, which were wonderful. Kinda gives me this song from the movie, ["The Last Goodbye"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q8ir8rVl2Z4), which was sung by one of the hobbit actors. Also, [Dermot Kennedy singing "The Parting Glass"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oH58PKlL3V8), which I believe is an old Irish traditional song that people would sing at the end of a gathering of friends. I just learned it actually has Scottish origins and ended up in Ireland somehow too.
