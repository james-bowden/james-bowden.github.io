## Please don't kiss me
#### James Bowden and Eve Blank

It is 4891 and I don't subscribe to the holiday<br/>
traditions of yore, anymore. New year, new me, my ass--<br/>
when's the last time you saw one of those <br/>
work? I am not new. I refuse to be new. I will soon see<br/>
a girl at the Hungarian pastry shop. No, we<br/>
haven't been. You will try to ask me who -- doesn't matter,<br/>
give it up, no, we aren't betrothed, life is <br/>
too short for that, but I need *somebody* to kiss me into <br/>
the new year. (If God really made us in His<br/>
image, why this gaping need? Wait--) I'm stuffing my face<br/>
full of apples, really, I write to forget that you<br/>
won't ever read this, where's the cider, fuck, that's right,<br/>
it evaporated last week along with my ability<br/>
to recall your blood type, I'd probably have remembered<br/>
had we been compatible, I guess. Along with<br/>

the narrative cohesion that keeps threatening<br/>
to accompany it, like a vampire at high noon,<br/>
like licking your teeth once more, or an amateur<br/>
poet pining on the new year's eve.<br/>
