## I Don't Want to Lose
#### Mary Oliver

I don't want to lose a single thread<br/>
from the intricate brocade of this happiness.<br/>
I want to remember everything. <br/>
Which is why I'm lying awake, sleepy<br/>
but not sleepy enough to give it up.<br/>
Just now, a moment from years ago:<br/>
the early morning light, the deft, sweet<br/>
gesture of your hand<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;reaching for me.<br/>

<hr>

This is another one of those poems that is literally spot-on for a feeling I have experienced.
