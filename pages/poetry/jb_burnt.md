## burnt edges
#### James Bowden

my brother insists that the burnt part of the noodle is the best to eat.<br/>
I tell him cancer is bad.<br/>

(<br/>
my brother insists the burnt part of the noodle is best.<br/>
I mean, listen, I cherish my burnt ends most of all--<br/>
I tell him cancer is *hard*.<br/>
he holds steady.<br/>

I love my burnt edges, burnt core, charcoal everything.<br/>
my friend's mom died 2 days after finding out she had cancer on her brain stem.<br/>
my brother holds steady, almost as steadily<br/>
as the cancer that took another friend's mom, who died many days after finding out she had brain cancer.<br/>

scale of objectivity, just as you are, I present you:<br/>
two friends, motherless, and some tasty noodles, nonetheless.<br/>
it's mothers' day. I pray for mine. on her side,<br/>
my aunt's only friend is so used to chemo that spring allergies casually coughed her ribs broken /<br/>

not to mention:<br/>
the burnt edges of my aunt's husband's blood cells, which leuked him out of skinny existence;<br/>
the burnt edges of my aunt's father's alveoli, life sucked sweetly out of existence like a cigaret;<br/>
the burnt edges of my aunt's mother's hair, singed by the same fires that crunched entire family to anon ash--<br/>
or was that from the car crash that winked her too out of existence, like dust mite astray the bin?<br/>
the burnt edges of my aunt's brother's chin, where friction caught like bullet like life fleeing holy brain;<br/>
just to be burnt once more and scattered to the sea.<br/>
)<br/>

burnt edges are yummy though!<br/>
*yes, yes. as you were*<br/>
(<br/>
the most beautiful part of your body,<br/>
edges delineated<br/>
)<br/>
*as you will*.<br/>
