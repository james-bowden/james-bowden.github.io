## Fleming Beach Initiation
#### James Bowden

And darling I know you’ve got grand plans for us<br/> 

Since the day we got back on that bus <br/>
Told me you’d be mine if I’d ask <br/>
Said we’d take it slow not fast <br/>

In your hair beneath that cold moonlight<br/> 
I barely thought to put up a fight <br/>
And into your willing arms I fell <br/>
Loving you more for having been through hell<br/>
 
So I mean it when I tell you that <br/>
I’ve loved you through the good and bad <br/>
Though my heart is caught up in my throat <br/>
I never meant to flood your boat <br/>

<hr>

This was an unfinished song? of sorts I wrote during my first instance of heartbreak, in large part inspired by Dermot Kennedy, so you're quite welcome to read it to yourself in his voice.
