## *As Light*
#### Devin Kelly

At night the old Russian farmer plows once more<br/>
the row of barley before whispering goodnight,<br/>
spokonoy nochi, to the tomato plant tilting liltingly<br/>
in the plot beside his cabin. & so it goes. This day<br/>
dims its shining to a simmer that simmers again<br/>
into the cool paste of dark. I am waiting to die<br/>
every day. It is okay. You are too. When it comes<br/>
I will not be ready. I am learning instead, a little bit<br/>
better, how to live. Come sunlight, the old Russian<br/>
wakes & whispers good morning through the small<br/>
crack of window. Dobroe utro, he murmurs toward<br/>
the plant, & the plant bows one near-purple & bulbous<br/>
tomato as if to greet him. It goes on like this, you<br/>
know. The greeting & the singing. Your lips pressed<br/>
against the cold. The finely-tuned harp the spider<br/>
plays beneath the surface. The people, & how they<br/>
came, & how you did not know what to say. Still<br/>
don't. So each morning you woke & spoke soft<br/>
to the easy listener that is the wall. & took photos<br/>
of light but did not know who to share them<br/>
with. Never knew what to make of the way this,<br/>
all of this, will go on without you. If it comforts<br/>
or if it harms. The old Russian farmer breaks<br/>
for lunch & holds the strawberry in his mouth<br/>
just a second longer each day. He holds it for hours<br/>
now. A lunch spent marinating his tongue in fruit.<br/>
He has earned it, but does not need to say this<br/>
to himself. As you might. As I most surely would.<br/>
He only sits in the high light of noon & makes<br/>
a kind of wine out of the inside of his mouth.<br/>
& smiles. Nods. Becomes, for a time, as close<br/>
to earth as God. Which it so say, so close to earth<br/>
he transcends it & allows himself to remake<br/>
the universe, over & over again, all of creation,<br/>
until the story goes that the moon rises & he knows<br/>
her name & says hello & the tomato plant beside<br/>
his cabin is the brother he lost in the war & each<br/>
strawberry, well, something about love, yes? Good<br/>
morning. The day is a memory we journey through<br/>
until we learn to call it home. In whatever language.<br/>
However long it takes. The people are here & we all<br/>
will go a little crazy. It happens. But this world<br/>
is good to us. I nod to the sky & it responds with<br/>
rain. I miss your kiss but find an old one you gave<br/>
trapped on the inside of my eyelid. I close my eyes<br/>
at night & somehow, for no reason, all these<br/>
pictures come to life. Sometimes they scare me.<br/>
That's okay. I wake & everything is changed without<br/>
my permission. How beautiful. Someone else calling<br/>
all these shots. & then I bend my head to the side,<br/>
confused, at times, by beauty. I once thought all good<br/>
things must end. But this doesn't. It goes on & on & on. & so I<br/>
was wrong. Nothing dies. It comes back as the old farmer,<br/>
the plant beside the cabin, the moon arcing at night<br/>
between the rough map of the stars, the fruit held in<br/>
the mouth, & yes, & yes, & yes. It comes back as light.<br/>
