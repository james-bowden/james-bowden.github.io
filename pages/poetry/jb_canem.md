## Historia ut Canem
#### James Bowden

I.<br/>
I’m domesticated, like a dog, and lick the palm of your hand. Your cheek. A rescue dog, a rescu*ed* dog, I <br/>
am feral underneath but I will learn to roll over and expose my soft underbelly: I will learn to do tricks.<br/>
