## Things I Don't Know What To Do With
#### James Bowden

1.
Sometimes I just don't know what to do with that. Like, *for what?* I saw Kristine today<br/>
and I mean, I've been over it and it's fine but still my heart burned and--<br/>
who said you were allowed to do this?<br/>

I miss feeling held.<br/>

It's been so long that I realize it's been forever.<br/><br/><br/>


2.
Weight like pressure like Atlas like stones call me Giles Corey, honey, in that voice of yours<br/>
I'm so fond of and perhaps I will accept this mantle, accept or be bludgeoned by, can I really<br/>
feel crushed with a gentle hand on my cheek and yeah I need more therapy don't you belabor it.<br/><br/><br/>


3.
I am one angsty angsty boy stability is not me<br/><br/><br/>


4.
I miss so much. I don't know what to say about it most of the time. I ask for more sweetness:<br/>
I am living in the cold. I miss anything I possibly can, as if <br/>
a sign that much to be missed shall be present again, something small to hold onto. <br/>
a pinky to grasp. an envelope to climb into. <br/><br/><br/>


5.
That poem about how an everyday common desolation can make you look up, notice beauty in the world. <br/>
Except here it's just random remnants that break my brain. Softness. Tenderness. A rock. <br/>
Beauty gone from the world, lingering like ticks.<br/>


6.
I am just so very full of emptiness these days, it doesn't make sense, so empty of fullness other than more<br/>
emptiness bulging at my seams, so much so that I don't know what to do with it. I mean, these are gradients:<br/>
I am just refusing to use them.<br/><br/><br/>


7.
life is really very odd.<br/>
i am become multitudinous.<br/>
i am become loving.<br/><br/><br/>


8.
This is always true, but: everything that happens is from now on,<br/>
and I continue to shift, I am the ground <br/>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;beneath myself. <br/>
All the world is just me interacting with myself, my fingers touch <br/>
my fingers, my words refract back at me, and so on. I am not in <br/>
love, but I am become a lover. All around me: attachment. My <br/>
breast becomes an open church for any soul that has suffered: <br/>
an open church for all lovers. One cannot suffer without loving<br/>
something, I should think. Yes, much of suffering is love of the<br/>
body, love of the self and such. But in a world where all I touch<br/>
is me—what else would it be? And is it odd that losing an <br/>
other is just losing part of self, some of my multitudes <br/>
detach or hide away, now pale vampires that at times <br/>
flow back out through my fingers: this is why they call<br/>
ink blood: this is why I must write. I come to adore the concrete,<br/>
not for its promises of permanence, but for its solidity even in its<br/>
transience. That is okay: turn to new sidewalks when the time comes:<br/>
you are fluid, stolid in your insolidity, you contain multitudes. It is not that you ARE<br/>
any of them or the conglomerate itself; no, you are the way they exist and move and die,<br/>
the how, the derivatives. Get in while you can. I am only here in the instant. As are you. Come. Open.<br/>

9.
Ábrete. Isn't that worth<br/>

something?<br/><br/><br/>
