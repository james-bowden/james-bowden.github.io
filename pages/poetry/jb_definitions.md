## Defintion Study
#### James Bowden

Say *rain*. Say *I love you*. <br/>

Sunday afternoon rain is falling.<br/>
All I can think of is you, lover of past, and<br/>
nothingness. So I'm back alive with only <br/>
3 weeks to be perfect.<br/>

Tears seep casually from my eyes.<br/>

Does rain become rain when it breaks from cloud, or as<br/>
it shatters itself on something below?<br/>
Is a relationship its first kiss, or its last?<br/>

Say *kiss*. No, say *kiss me*.<br/>

For a kiss to begin, the lips must part.<br/>
A kiss is defined by the sound of lips parting. <br/>
A kiss sounds only upon its ending.<br/>

I met a girl who kissed like a grasshopper. All lips,<br/>
no parting, no sound. Small, sterile, impersonal. <br/>
Flitting like wings. No lilt, no waves, no oscillation,<br/>
just contact. contact contact contact contact contact.<br/>
I didn't tell her. I never saw her again either, though.<br/><br/>


How to kiss:<br/>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;1. &nbsp;&nbsp;Think you might be able to love someone. Even the faintest sliver. Now stop thinking.<br/>
	&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2. &nbsp;&nbsp;Make eye contact. Ask politely, gently, smilingly.<br/>
	&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;3. &nbsp;&nbsp;Drink in their eyes, their everything. Now give up your sense of sight.<br/>
	&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4. &nbsp;&nbsp;Moisten your lips. This makes parting easier.<br/>
	&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;5. &nbsp;&nbsp;Lean in, close your eyes, open your lungs, then your lips, then your life<br/>
	&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;6. &nbsp;&nbsp;Wrap your fingers around whatever you can find blind, grasp for dear life--<br/>
	&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;7. &nbsp;&nbsp;open close open close open comma comma close gasp smile *OPEN* bite *gentle* close open close<br/><br/>
	
	
A kiss is an act of hope. To open once is to think that you shall open again.<br/>
To miss someone's kiss, then, is to miss opening. When you left,<br/>
a part of me closed. No amount of banging and pleading has opened it.<br/>
No battering ram determined enough has run me through.<br/>
To open more, close more. To love more, lose more.<br/><br/>


Say *relationship*. Say *us*. No, say *we*. <br/>

A relationship is an attachment.<br/>
We is a subject pronoun. We perform the action in a sentence, or we used to, at least.<br/>
We still do in my head.<br/>
Us is an object pronoun. We cannot receive the action of any verb,<br/>
for we are no longer an extant object.<br/>
We is transient. You get subbed out for my father, a friend, some coworkers, for a one-night stand.<br/>


I met a girl who kissed like a shark. All teeth,<br/>
as much parting as I could subtly manage, no sound,<br/>
just my lips being raked raw. Pain, metallic, <br/>
me perpetually pulling back in damage control. She<br/>
remarked that I seemed to really like kissing.<br/>
I didn't say anything, my lips visibly bloodied and bruised. <br/>
How hurt makes the body tender.<br/>

Say *tender*. Mean *soft*. Say<br/>
*I never miss you so much as when I try to open anew*.<br/>
Mean *again*; there is no such thing as *anew* now.<br/>

Say *flail*. Think *fail*, but mean *flail*.<br/>
My 7th-grade crush ate kale.<br/>
She had an iron deficiency. <br/>
Maybe shark-girl did, too.<br/>

Say *more food will equal more people. For us*.<br/>
Call me words you haven’t used since 7th-grade history. <br/>
Call me *arable8. Can the same raindrop fall twice? Is it the same<br/>
raindrop? Does it even matter?<br/>

I’ve changed so much already. When I say *I miss you*, <br/>
what I mean to say is, *I’m different*.<br/>

I've this image in my head of you looking across at me, bewildered by the cessation of kissing.<br/>
I just needed to catch my breath.<br/>

Say *open*. Say *close*. I want to know that I will be held again.<br/>
