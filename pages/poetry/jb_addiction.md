## I break my resolve and smoke weed again for the first time in over a month.
#### James Bowden

Sometimes—<br/>

Sometimes you just need to get it out of you. And sometimes it turns out that you just have an addictive personality, or are human, and under a lot of stress, and there’s nothing to get out: it’s just you.<br/> 

It’s just who you are under the given circumstances.<br/>
