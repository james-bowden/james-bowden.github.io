## Fake mint, or *I love you*
#### James Bowden 

How you walk through a place makes all the difference.<br/>

There's a difference between thinking someone will save you, and saving yourself through someone else.<br/>
There's a difference between noticing the moon beautiful and pointing it out to you.<br/>
How you walk through a place shapes the curves you fix to memory. <br/>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;you insist that it's mint, but its flowers circle orange and red concentricity.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;i try to tell you otherwise, but who am i to take home from a bee?<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;who am i to stomp pixie dust into plain old dirt?<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;just give me that popcorn chicken smile, darling<br/>

How I walk through you determines which side of my own headlights I am on.<br/>
I wish to wax gibbous til my eyes burst and all I can do is listen.<br/>
