## studying in the shadow of baxter basement
#### James Bowden

one green afternoon, quiet leaves 3 stories overhead<br/>

this morning, a tiny shoot poked out from the shelter of a broken mug<br/>
sown from her favorite fruit: beans<br/>
to think that I would write this /<br/>

she laughed in wonder, as i dozed off over my book of poems<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;over my open laptop<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;open homework<br/>
	&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;over the gentle hum of a woman dead of multiple (as if one wasn't enough) sclerae, seducing a cello into<br/>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the shape of Dvorak's famous 42-minute ejaculation, still not enough to hold her husband, her conductor<br/>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;no less!<br/>
edging me on, you are free, breathe, sleep, RUN!<br/>
one green afternoon, quiet leaves 3 stories overhead<br/>
