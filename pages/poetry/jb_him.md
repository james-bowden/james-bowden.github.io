## Him
#### James Bowden

Insistent upon his ability <br/>
To change <br/>
Stony <br/>
Brooding <br/>
Resolute, yet aware<br/> 
Nonchalant <br/>
Dismissive <br/>
Everything he represses <br/>
Nothing he stands for<br/> 
Caught <br/>
No, dwelling <br/>
In limbo <br/>
Too stubborn to be.<br/>

<hr>

Again, I think I've greatly departed from this, but here it is as an artifact. I think the repressing vs. standing for dichotomy is always in part true for many of us, but hopefully I've gotten better at being open about things.
