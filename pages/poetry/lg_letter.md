## The Letter
#### Linda Gregg

I’m not feeling strong yet, but I am taking<br/>
good care of myself. The weather is perfect.<br/>
I read and walk all day and then walk to the sea.<br/>
I expect to swim soon. For now I am content.<br/>
I am not sure what I hope for. I feel I am<br/>
doing my best. It reminds me of when I was<br/>
sixteen dreaming of Lorca, the gentle trees outside<br/>
and the creek. Perhaps poetry replaces something<br/>
in me that others receive more naturally.<br/>
Perhaps my happiness proves a weakness in my life.<br/>
Even my failures in poetry please me.<br/>
Time is very different here. It is very good<br/>
to be away from public ambition.<br/>
I sweep and wash, cook and shop.<br/>
Sometimes I go into town in the evening<br/>
and have pastry with custard. Sometimes I sit<br/>
at a table by the harbor and drink half a beer.<br/>
