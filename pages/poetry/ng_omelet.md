I mainly included this poem as an interesting way of writing a poem about writing this poem itself. This one is less about describing art at all, but the experience and speaker's frazzled mind after it serve to create the art, and really this sort of experience is of a similar kind to what we often get out of impactful art.

## I Wrote a Good Omelet
#### Nikki Giovanni


I wrote a good omelet...and ate<br/>
a hot poem... after loving you<br/>
Buttoned my car...and drove my<br/>
coat home...in the rain...<br/>
after loving you<br/>
I goed on red...and stopped on<br/>
green...floating somewhere in between...<br/>
being here and being there...<br/>
after loving you<br/>
I rolled my bed...turned down<br/>
my hair...slightly<br/>
confused but...I don't care...<br/>
Laid out my teeth...and gargled my<br/>
gown...then I stood<br/>
...and laid me down...<br/>
To sleep...<br/>
after loving you<br/>


---
I think what this poem is doing is pretty straightforward, so will leave it at that.
