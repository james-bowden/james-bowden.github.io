## Prodigal lover
#### James Bowden 

It rained for three days straight the week that you left. That I left. That we left. <br/>
That I tried to leave. <br/>

And I’d rather be the prodigal son than the perfect one <br/>
For in this brief life, what good is it to be home if you’ve never been lost?<br/>
