## *tell them we're young mastodons*
#### James Bowden

you--where all others have failed--have<br/>
begun to elicit me. convince me. compel<br/>
me. draw me out of me. i find that with<br/>
you--surrender becomes me, feels  good?<br/>

we meet. we chat. we sit up hugging the<br/>
plushies in my room, keeping our 5 foot<br/>
distance. i am meek. you let me walk you<br/>
back. i steer us toward the longer route.<br/>

we walk we talk we joke we poke<br/>
we hug we bug we romp we stomp<br/><br/>

<hr>

The title is one of my favorite lyrics (to be read in that tone! important!!) from Bon Iver's *Naeem*. I think the overall tone is inspired a bit by Mark Strand's ["Eating Poetry"](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/52959/eating-poetry).
