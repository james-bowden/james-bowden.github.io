## smallness study #2
#### James Bowden 

Your lips braced against the cold<br/>
My buttocks braced against the porcelain toilet seat<br/>
My lips taut against my teeth<br/>
Heart pushing against my chest<br/>
A small moment, though it has been this whole<br/>
morning, of opening: of realizing love is possible.<br/>
That it has already begun to exist, without my<br/>
permission. Despite the old defenses. Despite my<br/>
silly self. My silly little self.

<hr>

The ending is inspired in form by Mary Oliver; the first line by Devin Kelly (in "As Light"); and the repetition feels like Cameron Awkward-Rich's "Meditations in an Emergency".
