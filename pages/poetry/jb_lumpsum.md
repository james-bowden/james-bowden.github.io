## Low-fidelity ekphrastic nod to "Lump Sum"
#### James Bowden

Sold my cold knot, a heavy stone<br/>
Stop what I'm doing, lay on the floor for 3:21<br/>
This, a pleasant surprise whilst trying to understand mortgage payments<br/>
Grain, graininess, an ailing man's voice, cracking, speakers, rain, heat, father--<br/>
How can a song, how can a person, instantly become nostalgic?<br/>
To wake up already dead.<br/>

<hr>

This poem takes inspiration as well as some words from Bon Iver's song "Lump Sum" and the comments section from the [youtube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eCSi8OU0gZc).
