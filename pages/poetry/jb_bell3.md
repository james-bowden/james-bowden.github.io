##Poem Number Three on Bell’s Theorem, or The New New Physicality of Long Distance Love
#### James Bowden

We will probably fall apart--<br/>
I am horny. <br/>
There are hot singles in my area.<br/>

<hr>

This series of poems was generally inspired in form by June Jordan's short poem, ["Poem Number Two on Bell’s Theorem, or The New Physicality of Long Distance Love"](https://poets.org/poem/poem-number-two-bells-theorem-or-new-physicality-long-distance-love).<br/>
This one in particular is meant to come right after the original Number Two poem linked above. There's an alternative version, ["Poem Number Four ..."](https://james-bowden.github.io/pages/poetry/jb_bell4.md). And a precursor version, ["Poem Number One ..."](https://james-bowden.github.io/pages/poetry/jb_newton1.md).
