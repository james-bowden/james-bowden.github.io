## Nothings
#### James Bowden

I’m lying in bed, facedown past noon. <br/>
There are times when nothing means anything.<br/> 
No word, no sound, no thought enough to move me<br/>
When nothing but a soft hug will do, <br/>
preferably with a little nuzzle. <br/>
And soon. <br/><br/>

Of course, it is in these times that I think of you. <br/>
Nothing can be done, so I close my eyes and go to.<br/>

<hr>

I liked the rhyming in this little poem of sorts.
