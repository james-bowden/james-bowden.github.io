## Tea for You, Too
#### Ron Padgett

My friends,<br/>
I want to tell you<br/>
that in general things<br/>
are all right with me,<br/>
relatively speaking.<br/>
Just a second, here's Einstein<br/>
asking where the tea is.<br/>
I reassure him<br/>
it will be ready soon,<br/>
relatively speaking,<br/>
as he shuffles back<br/>
to the room that holds him,<br/>
with plenty of space<br/>
for that cup of tea,<br/>
even though the cup<br/>
is twelve feet in diameter,<br/>
about the same size<br/>
as my thinking of you<br/>
this morning.<br/>
