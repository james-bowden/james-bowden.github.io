## strawberry \*shit\*
#### James Bowden

*for Antonia*

Being made—<br/>

Don’t you get it? It’s the parrot trembling on the orange tree outside.<br/>
It’s chef snoopy.<br/>
It’s buying salmon skins that I wouldn’t eat if you paid me to and all the times we might’ve died. <br/>
Cat parade, Lip Parade, for emma, forever ago. Going Costco chicken on some homemade curry and soup.<br/>
It’s the corner, and how we’re always living in the cold until we’re not, and to be warm is to make way<br/>
for a deeper chill and by golly, how I shiver with liveliness already. The way snow<br/>
touches the skin, and is “snow” more. Oh, Ocean, this is all but sputtering;<br/> 
my crayons are smearing and there are no real words for that<br/>

Perhaps, most of all,<br/>
it is the strawberry plant<br/>
that won’t berry by the time you’ve gone<br/>

and how little difference it makes anyway, how local life can be<br/>

and as you would say:<br/>
\*shit\*<br/>

<hr>

There are myriad references here, to a Studio Ghibli movie called *The Cat Returns*, Bon Iver's *Holocene* as well as *For Emma, Forever Ago*, Dermot Kennedy's *The Corner* as well as *Young and Free*, and of course, Ocean Vuong's poem "Devotion". And Antonia.
