## God damn it.
#### James Bowden

I hate Texas. It’s always hot. The sun burns through my skin, sapping me of my energy. Funny how that works. <br/>
Water. Cold water. <br/>
I take a sip from the dented orange Nalgene and taste the rubber of the splash guard. <br/>
Still the best purchase I’ve ever made. <br/>
And it still tastes as it did 6 summers ago, when I bought it full of coke for $14 at Camp Whitsett <br/>
I think <br/>

I remember swimming that mile in the frigid mountain lake. <br/>
Watching Johnny nearly cut his fingers off and Alec Moya eat moths off of the lanterns. <br/>
Carving some totem out of wood. I don’t even know what the fuck I made. <br/>
Watching Forrest Gump for the first time. <br/>
Whistling obnoxiously with Keith as the flag climbed each morning. <br/>
Cooking cheeseburgers and shooting guns. <br/>
Tipping the mast of my sailboat over and right into the mud at the bottom of the lake. Or was that at Emerald<br/>
Bay? Or the second year at Whitsett? Was there even a second year at Whitsett? <br/>

I don’t fucking know. <br/>
I know that it happened. That I was there. At some point in time. <br/>
But I do not remember. <br/>
I cannot. <br/>
And you are absent from those memories <br/>
More like historical facts. <br/>
And yet the only bit I truly know, truly remember with every cell in my body <br/>
Is that I loved you then <br/>
And that I long for you now <br/>
Carrying patrol boxes and burning rice and rapping Lighters at midnight and pouring our souls out into each<br/>
other’s hands as shooting stars blinked across the horizon until our tyrannical eyelids finally conquered our tired minds <br/>

I don’t know what the fuck we talked about. <br/>
I never did the next morning. <br/>
Only that I wished I didn’t get tired so that we could spend every minute of those 45 hours <br/>
Together. Being. <br/>
And now I remember:<br/>
The only real hug we ever shared up on that rock as the kids smoked weed in the premature mountain blackness <br/>
The silver lining. <br/>
Sanding those pinewood blocks into shitty cars when no one else would join us <br/>
Playing cards. <br/>
Eating Doritos and Oreos knowing the consequences of tomorrow and not caring <br/>
Sleeping side by side, warmly floating in that cold dark and not because of our hammocks <br/>

You say I had no childhood. <br/>
Because I never wasted time. <br/>
Because I had never seen the shows you’d seen, because I did not play the games that you did, because you<br/>
could choose any movie from your hard drive and I’d not have watched it. <br/>
You are wrong. <br/>
You were my childhood. <br/>
God damn you. For making me feel. For teaching me love. <br/>

Perhaps my Nalgene doesn’t taste like rubber anymore. It’s been washed hundreds of times since I bought it. <br/>
But the taste on my tongue is no less real and I am sure of it regardless of whether it truly is or not. Now. <br/>
Fuck Texas. I might as well be on Venus.<br/>

This holds for this and a good amount of the older poetry here, but also in general: I won't really speak for the quality of the poetry, but I think some of the sentiments are nice and it's also nice as a record of what I was thinking and feeling and writing back then, when I was younger and such.
