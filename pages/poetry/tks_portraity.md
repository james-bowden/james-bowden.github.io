## *Self-Portrait as the Letter Y*
#### Tracy K. Smith

1.<br/>

I waved a gun last night<br/>
In a city like some ancient Los Angeles.<br/>
It was dusk. There were two girls<br/>
I wanted to make apologize,<br/>
But the gun was uselessly heavy.<br/>
They looked sideways at each other<br/>
And tried to flatter me. I was angry.<br/>
I wanted to cry. I wanted to bury the pistol,<br/>
But I would’ve had to walk miles.<br/>
I would’ve had to learn to run.<br/>

 

2.<br/>

 

I have finally become that girl<br/>
In the photo you keep among your things,<br/>
Steadying myself at the prow of a small boat.<br/>
It is always summer here, and I am<br/>
Always staring into the lens of your camera,<br/>
Which has not yet been stolen. Always<br/>
With this same expression. Meaning<br/>
I see your eye behind the camera’s eye.<br/>
Meaning that in the time it takes<br/>
For the tiny guillotine<br/>
To open and fall shut, I will have decided<br/>
I am just about ready to love you.<br/>

 

3.<br/>

 

Sun cuts sharp angles<br/>
Across the airshaft adjacent.<br/>

 

They kiss. They kiss again.<br/>
Faint clouds pass, disband.<br/>

 

Someone left a mirror<br/>
At the foot of the fire escape.<br/>

 

They look down. They kiss.<br/>
She will never be free<br/>

 

Because she is afraid. He<br/>
Will never be free<br/>

 

Because he has always<br/>
Been free.<br/>

 

4.<br/>

 

Was kind of a rebel then.<br/>
Took two cars. Took<br/>
Bad advice. Watched people’s<br/>
Asses. Sniffed their heads.<br/>

 

Just left, so it looked<br/>
Like those half sad cookouts,<br/>
Meats never meant to be<br/>
Flayed, meant nothing.<br/>

 

Made promises. Kept going.<br/>
Prayed for signs. Stooped<br/>
For coins. Needed them.<br/>
Had two definitions of family.<br/>

 

Had two families. Snooped.<br/>
Forgot easily. Well, didn’t<br/>
Forget, but knew when it was safe<br/>
To remember. Woke some nights<br/>

 

Against a wet pillow, other nights<br/>
With the lights on, whispering<br/>
The truest things<br/>
Into the receiver.<br/>

 

5.<br/>

 

A small dog scuttles past, like a wig<br/>
Drawn by an invisible cord. It is spring.<br/>
The pirates out selling fakes are finally<br/>
Able to draw a crowd. College girls,<br/>
Inspired by the possibility of sex,<br/>
Show bare skin in good faith. They crouch<br/>
Over heaps of bright purses, smiling,<br/>
Willing to pay. Their arms<br/>
Swing forward as they walk away, balancing<br/>
That new weight on naked shoulders.<br/>
The pirates smile, too, watching<br/>
Pair after pair of thighs carved in shadow<br/>
As girl after girl glides into the sun.<br/>

 

6.<br/>

 

You are pure appetite. I am pure<br/>
Appetite. You are a phantom<br/>
In that far-off city where daylight<br/>
Climbs cathedral walls, stone by stolen stone.<br/>
I am invisible here, like I like it.<br/>
The language you taught me rolls<br/>
From your mouth into mine<br/>
The way kids will pass smoke<br/>
Between them. You feed it to me<br/>
Until my heart grows fat. I feed you<br/>
Tiny black eggs. I feed you<br/>
My very own soft truth. We believe.<br/>
We stay up talking all kinds of shit<br/>
