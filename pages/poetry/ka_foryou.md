## For You
#### Kim Addonizio

For you I undress down to the sheaths of my nerves.<br/>
I remove my jewelry and set it on the nightstand,<br/>
I unhook my ribs, spread my lungs flat on a chair.<br/>
I dissolve like a remedy in water, in wine.<br/>
I spill without staining, and leave without stirring the air.<br/>
I do it for love. For love, I disappear.<br/>
