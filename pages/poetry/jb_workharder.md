## workharder16
#### James Bowden

the way i recited "Invictus" to myself as a child.<br/>
the way my passwords were, and regrettably still are "workharder16"--<br/>
	&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;symbolizing first the obvious and second the favorite number of my 7th grade lost love,<br/>
	&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;and it is almost as if you can see right through me, that there is reason more than just email access to keep this password a secret--<br/>
the way i printed "Invictus" out and immortalized it in plastic sleeve upon my bedroom wall.<br/>
the way i had it memorized, and still recall <br/>
	&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*I am the master of my fate: I am the captain of my soul*<br/>
the way i am just now learning to allow emotions in, to forgive myself<br/>

my younger brother inherited the room and keeps the printout there and that mortifies me.<br/>

<hr>

Obviously not anymore, but you're welcome to try, I suppose.
