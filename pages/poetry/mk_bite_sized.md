## [i wasted my teenage years shrinking myself into bite sized pieces]
#### Muskan K?

i wasted my teenage years shrinking myself into bite sized pieces-<br/>
i have never known anything else.<br/>
my fingers are blue and are fading to a violent purple,<br/>
this body feels out of place like a fallen domino-<br/>
when will summer feel less like winter reborn,<br/>
and more like relief?<br/>
there's a clock in my mind<br/>
reminding me that time has stolen my youth,<br/>
and that im both better and far worse than i was before.<br/>
