## So what? And?
#### James Bowden

And so what if I love you in spite of myself <br/>
of her, of my present confines <br/>
And so what if all I want is to curl up next to you, inside of you, your head on my chest, my cheek in the small<br/>
of your hand, my heart under your foot and yours in my chest <br/>
And wake up next to you, playfully slinging clothes on and off, a shallow disguise for what we are and <br/>
crave to be <br/>
And so what if I am only dreaming and we’ve hardly talked, much less seen each other in months <br/>
And so what if you are a part of my past, and I yours, though we both know the former is a lie, and never<br/>
collide again we shall <br/>
For I know that you are what I want, and not what I have, and next time, I won’t let you slip by <br/>
I was never good in broad daylight but in the shadows I do love.<br/>

<hr>

These days, I say, *why not broad daylight?*, and indeed, that has been much of what I've been working on doing. I think it comes through well in a lot of the later poems.
