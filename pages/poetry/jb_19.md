## Nineteen
#### James Bowden

And it was a great morning, if ever you could call a morning that<br/>
For the sun shined and I woke up alive--if not a tad sleep deprived<br/>
But surely nonetheless.<br/>

Nineteen, they say.<br/>
Another year gone, I hear.<br/>
And I feel it too, in my back, in my bones, in my thoughts, in this frail vessel, <br/>
as surely as I've ever felt anything.<br/>

I feel it in the way I crack my knuckles, twisting and bending my fingers <br/>
fluidly without a second of thought confident that they wont break<br/>
savoring the release of my thumb and finding comfort in the reliable<br/>
reaction of my neck as I snap it--gently, of course--<br/>
and ending in my ankles, my toes, my toes, my toes…and my toes<br/>
I am solid in my fluidity, at least in this sense. And what a magnanimous comfort that is.<br/>

I'm sure of it in the way I sigh deeply upon consideration,<br/>
as cathartic as laying me down to rest<br/>
Don't forget the jaw.<br/>
or feeling the weak stream of warm water fill my airholes as it does my pores. *Flush*,<br/><br/>


I thrive in it as I lace negatives and double negatives and I dont knows together<br/>
into spears, driven through the hearts of insecurity and uncertainty<br/>
And since when was having the power to slay something indicative of separation and superiority?<br/>
for I certainly have none<br/>
And since when did I deserve to be capitalized--no, reduce me back to what i truly am--<br/>
delusional, yes, but sane all the same and surely more as human as the rest for it<br/>

How becoming.<br/>
Ain't it scary that I must become?<br/>
Or perhaps a more frightening thought is that I already have become, whether I like it or believe it or not.<br/>
And such is life. But it'll be good. No doubt.<br/>
And so I go to, or should I say, go too?<br/><br/>


And I die in it in the way that I attempt to share it<br/>
for how can another understand what worries them not<br/>
and why would one worry over what they've contemplated not<br/>
and for what would one brood when plague them thoughts do not<br/>
i don't know. <br/>
Perhaps it is the absence of thoughts that plagues me.<br/>

Still. I can avoid becoming about as well as I can avoid the divots in my personality for to become not would be to become not, not to not become. <br/>
no, weave--no, spin.<br/>
Try. I know that life is all about the wind, and try I still do. *Well*--<br/>
Is a trap a trap if willingly I fall in?<br/>

And what is death, but the permanent quiescence of the mind?<br/>
And what then must be life?<br/>
For in truth, what more could I ask for than to be <br/>
mortal.<br/>
