## For Antonia, next letter:
#### James Bowden

June Jordan has this poem that goes, *I will not* <br/>
*love those who do not love me, I will be nobody’s* <br/>
*fool*, and so on, I used to chuckle at its overly cynical<br/>
and cringe just a little bit, but this has made me consider<br/>
it more seriously.<br/>
I resolved not to text people who wouldn’t text me back, this year,<br/>
and I suppose that includes you at present. Nothing is that deep. <br/>
I harbor hurt, but not resentment.<br/>
Godspeed you (toward wherever you go).<br/>
