## Splash of Rain: a Yankee Candle scent
#### James Bowden

So this is how I am to mourn--<br/>

with the sweet scent of rose jam,<br/>
the exfoliating burn,<br/>
silent warm rain and shower thoughts<br/>

and snapchats and instagram posts<br/>
and poetry thrown into the void<br/>

unpopped popcorn and untouched bracelet string<br/>

the eternal you on my mind<br/>

shall I split me<br/>
from what is you?<br/>
can I?<br/>

is this me choosing short term james<br/>
over long term longing,<br/>
or perhaps, it is really you<br/>
who is choosing not to choose<br/>

some summer night,<br/>
some rainy morning<br/>
I hope I see you again<br/>

--you, my youthful eagerness<br/>
as if the two could be separated<br/>
