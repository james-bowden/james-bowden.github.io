## love version of
#### Richard Scott

tonight I watched you sleep<br/>
naked on the futon<br/>
face down sweaty like a small child<br/>
and knew that everything else was bullshit<br/>

it's so hard to stay alive these days<br/>
or sane<br/>
so keep on snoring danny<br/>
while I guard you like a rottweiler<br/>

being in love with you is fucking awful<br/>
cause one day you'll stop breathing<br/>
in this grey light you already look dead<br/>

but then you smile thank fuck<br/>
what are you dreaming about baby wake up<br/>
tell me if the word soul still means anything<br/>
