## Life as AI PhD Student
#### James Bowden

I wake up when I can no longer<br/>
sleep. Some days I'm fortunate<br/>
and eat my breakfast on the balcony.<br/>
My oats cold on my tongue. I close<br/>
my eyes. I close my eyes and sit on<br/>
the warm leather of my rocking chair<br/>
and I stare into the sun. I can see<br/>
nothing and still my breath slowly slows<br/>
my squint to a calm closure the sun<br/>
it's so warm its so warm the sun they<br/>
dont they cant know im all alone and<br/>
in the sun oh marx oh feuerbach oh me<br/>
oh my oh what little is mine<br/>
