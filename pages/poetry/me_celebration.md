## Celebration
#### Mari Evans

I will bring you a whole person<br/>
and you will bring me a whole person<br/>
and we will have us twice as much<br/>
of love and everything<br/>

I be bringing a whole heart<br/>
and while it do have nicks and<br/>
dents and scars,<br/>
that only make me lay it down<br/>
more careful-like<br/>
An’ you be bringing a whole heart<br/>
a little chipped and rusty an’<br/>
sometime skip a beat but<br/>
still an’ all you bringing polish too<br/>
and look like you intend<br/>
to make it shine<br/>

And we be bringing, each of us<br/>
the music of ourselves to wrap<br/>
the other in<br/>

Forgiving clarities<br/>
Soft as a choir’s last<br/>
lingering note our<br/>
personal blend<br/>

I will be bringing you someone whole<br/>
and you will be bringing me someone whole<br/>
and we be twice as strong<br/>
and we be twice as true<br/>
and we will have twice as much<br/>
of love<br/>
and everything<br/>
