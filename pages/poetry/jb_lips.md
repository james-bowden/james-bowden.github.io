## I lick my lips and write with my tongue
#### James Bowden

Lips as smooth as summer rain<br/>
Cheeks like milky bread<br/>

Strands of silken spiders' web<br/>
Fall neatly round his head<br/>

This to say 'tis easy to love the beauty of his skin<br/>
This a gleaming guise to hide the turmoil lie'n within<br/>
