Welcome--I'm beginning this blog of sorts as a place to organize and keep some of the poetry that I process each day. This is a personal project, and not particularly for you, so do keep that in mind, though it'd be a pretty coincidence if you do find something to love.
I want to state here very clearly that pretty much all of the poems here are not mine, and respective credit is due to their authors and publishers and such. I have set them here usually because I could not find any satisfactory online version of them, and only intend to share pieces that I have loved.
