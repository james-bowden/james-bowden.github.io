## *I Wrote a Good Omelet*
#### Nikki Giovanni


I wrote a good omelet...and ate<br/>
a hot poem... after loving you<br/>
Buttoned my car...and drove my<br/>
coat home...in the rain...<br/>
after loving you<br/>
I goed on red...and stopped on<br/>
green...floating somewhere in between...<br/>
being here and being there...<br/>
after loving you<br/>
I rolled my bed...turned down<br/>
my hair...slightly<br/>
confused but...I don't care...<br/>
Laid out my teeth...and gargled my<br/>
gown...then I stood<br/>
...and laid me down...<br/>
To sleep...<br/>
after loving you<br/>
