The funny thing about this poem, having read both of its precursors, is that this genuinely did mark something of a turning point in my life, like Mark Leidner describes [here](https://poetrysociety.org/features/in-their-own-words/on-having-having-a-coke-with-you-with-you). Namely, it was one of my first times genuinely engaging with art in the sense that I loved this poem and its characters so much that I found myself thinking about it often and eventually emulating it as follows. Enjoy!

## *Having "Having "Having a Coke with You" with You" with You*
#### James Bowden

is nothing special, but also everything special, I'm sorry, I oscillate between the two;<br/>
you had just sent me a poem called "Celebration" that I'd posted, thinking of you, of course<br/>
about love, and you said it was interesting how differently we'd interpreted it, that you were tempted<br/>
to send this to "new boy", the only name we dared give him, but weren't sure<br/>
if you'd stopped yourself partly because you were afraid to hand your heart on a platter to someone, <br/>
or partly because part of you sees sharing poems as something special just between you and me<br/>
and I, who hands my hearts on platters to anyone I possibly can, as early and as late as permitted,<br/>
asked you if you had seen this poem and that I absolutely adored it, and you responded a minute later<br/>
saying you were just about to send it to me actually, since you'd seen it independently <br/>
(but dependently, because you'd started following poetry accounts because I'd shared so many poems)<br/>
and you felt I'd memorize a poem just to ask someone their thoughts on it <br/>
and I awfully loved us both in that moment.<br/>
anyhow, I continued encouraging you to love him despite your fear of not having heart space <br/>
for us both, and talked about how I was going to bake an apple pie with "new girl" and<br/>
watch the hobbit and offer a piece of pie to "old girl", who had deeply hurt me weeks before by<br/>
sleeping with my best friend without the customary 2 weeks' notice, and whom I had<br/>
meant to go to in anger before I talked with you on the phone and all vitriol fled my heart<br/>
and the only thing I could fathom doing was hugging my hurt and love into each of them<br/>
before taking some countably indefinite amount of space, and never had my heart felt so full--<br/>
I still send you poems now and then, though you won't engage, so there's your answer to that,<br/>
and I think about all the having with you that I no longer can do<br/>
and I knew I wanted to be with you forever a year before having this poem with you, but just last night<br/>
I dreamt of you again and--how tender you were--I felt lost with your new boy there, as<br/>
will likely happen if I visit this summer and as happened last summer though realize it I did not,<br/>
but you smiled upon me with such warmth that I knew I was at the last line of the poem.<br/>
