Here's an attempt of mine at a poem describing some artistic experiences in a somewhat narrative progression.

## *I read to save my soul*
#### James Bowden


I crumple onto my bed<br/>
task lists flying round my head<br/>
and settling, landing, bleary upon my breast<br/>

I poke open youtube<br/>
shudder past last night's bon iver<br/>
to get to the song whose meter <br/>
my internal dialogue this whole week<br/>

a little boy in boxing shoes: HAM ON RYE<br/>
I thumb through worn pages, neck smeared with sky<br/>
til it all collapses in and I am left to <br/>

two nouns, the best sentence I've run today:<br/>
*I'm pretty sure I just impulse-bought depression*<br/>
don't worry--she's used.<br/>


---
The song mentioned as internal dialogue is ["Presumably Dead Arm"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EtEFpVPhvco)--you can probably see how the flow of the song might parametrize one's thoughts. The book in question is Charles Bukowski's [*Ham on Rye*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ham_on_Rye). This poem was written before this anthology was conceived of, which goes to show how prevalent this topic was whether I realized it or not.
