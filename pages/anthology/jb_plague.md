## *a plague upon my brain*
#### James Bowden

<br/>
<p style="text-align: right; font-style: italic;"> Submitting someone else's text as one's own or attempting to blur the line between one's own ideas or words and those borrowed from another source<br/>
--Kent State University<br/><br/>
 </p>


plagiarism--that nasty bastard!<br/>
explicitly sworn nemesis<br/>
(keep your enemies closest)<br/>
of third-graders and career academicians alike,<br/>
affront to honor, honesty, hubris!<br/>
*Thoughtcrime!, Ideatheft!* I cry,<br/>
and set me out to write something original:<br/>

I am a writer. You ask for a poem, an original one.<br/>
I offer you a blade of grass. You say it is not good enough,<br/>
so I offer you a poem about a blade of grass woven into<br/>
mine. It is enough for me, and it will be enough for you.<br/>

You extend me half-loaf of bread; will you really be offended should I chew it up, <br/>
hold it years in mouth, a kind of grass-dough waiting?<br/>
I eat of your body that others may eat of mine.<br/>
Thinketh thou that Jesus meant his flesh to stop at his twelve disciples?<br/>

I mean, look. I am a neural network. I read everything. I own you.<br/>
I backprop you, and take you, preprocessed, into me.<br/>
You ask me to generate something new, but there is no chance <br/>
you are not part of it--there are no parts.<br/>
*To have a door*, you say.<br/>
*To be a door*, I reply.<br/><br/>
